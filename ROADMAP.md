# Asha Yojana - Growth Roadmap (approved by owner, 28 Nov 2026)

Vision: ashayojana.com is the most trusted, simple-Hindi place for sarkari yojana, documents, money and education help.
The site is dedicated to the owner's mother, Asha ji. Trust and accuracy are the brand. Never trade them for speed.

Honest note: "name everywhere" is built in months, not days. We grow by being useful and correct, not by spam. Targets below are goals, not predictions.

## What Claude does automatically (daily run, see AGENT.md)
- Parts A-E every day: new scheme pages, refreshed old pages, 2 khabar posts, 2-3 guides, FAQs/deadlines upkeep.
- 2-3 ready-to-paste WhatsApp share posts in SHARE_TODAY.md.
- Weekly traffic report (Monday) and weekly health check (Sunday) already scheduled.
- Content follows the TOPIC MAP in AGENT.md (3 active clusters: documents, money/bank, education).

## What only the owner can do (small, repeat weekly)
1. Paste the daily share posts into the WhatsApp channel (10 min/day).
2. Create and keep active: Telegram channel, Facebook page, Instagram, YouTube (same name "Asha Yojana", same logo).
3. Check AdSense "Sites" status; send message if "Needs attention".
4. Send a Search Console export every 4 weeks (Queries + Pages).
5. Create the Google Form for contact (then Claude wires FORM_URL).
6. Decide on the Asha ji story page (see Brand).

## Phase 0 - Foundation (now to 15 Dec 2026)
- AdSense review pending; do not touch ads.txt/ad code; no self-clicks.
- Search Console: sitemap submitted, check Pages report for "Discovered / not indexed"; use URL Inspection to request indexing for the 10 newest guides.
- Contact Google Form -> replace email buttons.
- Karnataka e-KYC and other dated news updated after deadlines.
- Fix anything broken found by the weekly health check.
- KPI check (15 Dec): indexed pages, impressions/day, clicks/week.

## Phase 1 - Depth (Dec 2026 to Feb 2027): earn Google's trust
- Content: 60+ guides across the 3 clusters, each with sources, steps, documents, tips and honest "could not verify" hedges. Every state page links to its guides.
- Hubs: build /documents, /money, /education hub pages (pillar pages linking all micro niches). Add breadcrumbs and internal links from every scheme to related guides.
- Tools (what AI answers cannot do): loan EMI (done), education-loan EMI, pension estimator (verified states only), per-scheme document checklist, scholarship eligibility finder, "will my payment come" checklist.
- Trust pages (E-E-A-T): About with owner/editor name and the story of the site, Editorial policy ("how we verify" exists), Corrections log page (every fix dated), visible "last verified" date on every page.
- Freshness: khabar posts daily; deadlines page kept current; weekly page rebuilt.
- Discover: keep 1200x630 images on every page; avoid clickbait.
- KPI goals (aim, not promise): Dec 1,000 impressions/day, Jan 3,000, Feb 8,000; clicks follow as titles/FAQs improve.

## Phase 2 - Distribution (Jan to Mar 2027): make people share and mention us
- WhatsApp channel + Telegram: daily 2-3 posts; one "Is hafte ki deadlines" post every Monday (from weekly page).
- Printable branded checklists (PDF/print page) with the site name: owners of CSC / Jan Seva Kendras, cyber cafes, ASHA/anganwadi workers and panchayat helpers can print and use them. This is the best offline brand builder.
- YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels (30-45 s, Hinglish voiceover) from guides: "paisa nahi aaya? 3 check", "ration card e-KYC kaise karein". Claude can prepare scripts; owner posts.
- Honest outreach for links: CSC groups, college placement/scholarship cells, NGO resource lists, local news sites, Hindi educators. Offer them our checklist, not a paid link. No link buying, no comment spam.
- Google News Publisher Center: apply once khabar has 60+ original posts and a clear editorial policy page.
- Answer questions on Quora/Reddit/Facebook groups only where it genuinely helps, with the link as source.

## Phase 3 - Reach (Mar to Sep 2027)
- Regional languages (largest unused lever in India): start with the biggest state-language pages (Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati) for state schemes and top guides. Must be proofread; machine-only text is not published.
- Open the next clusters: Health, Kisan and gaon, Jobs/exams "how to check" (no live dates), Senior citizens.
- PWA / installable site and push notifications (OneSignal exists) for deadline alerts.
- Evaluate Cloudflare/CDN when traffic needs it (reminder trigger set for 22 Dec 2026).
- Monetisation beyond AdSense only if safe: sponsored posts are NOT accepted if they promote agents, loan apps or anything that could mislead users.

## Brand (name, face, story)
- One name: "Asha Yojana" (ashayojana.com), one logo (leaf + diya), one tagline "सरकारी योजनाएँ, आसान भाषा में" everywhere.
- Dedication: "माँ आशा जी को समर्पित" already on the home page.
- OPTIONAL (owner decides): an "Asha ji ki kahani" page on About telling why the site exists (and, if he wants, the Asha Techno Management Institute chapter). Nothing personal is published without his exact wording and approval.
- Never: fake testimonials, fake numbers, copied content, "agent se karwao" style links.

## Quality rules that protect the name (never relax)
- Verify with 2+ sources, official where readable; hedge or drop unverified facts; log "could not verify".
- No helpline numbers/URLs/fees unless read on an official page.
- Fix wrong facts quickly and note the correction.
- Original writing only; no scraping.

## Monthly review (first Monday of the month)
Claude reports: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, top queries with high impressions/low clicks, pages to improve, content coverage by cluster/micro niche, AdSense status (from owner), and the next month's plan. Roadmap is updated after each review.
