Government Scheme Money Not Received? How to Check PFMS / DBT Payment Status and Where to Complain

📤 Share:

Check these 3 things first: (1) your bank account is active and KYC is complete; (2) your Aadhaar is mapped to this account in NPCI; (3) the bank name, IFSC and account number are correct and identical on the form and passbook. A problem in any one of them can hold up the payment. Then: when a scheme instalment or subsidy does not show in your account, first find out whether the money was sent at all, and into which account. PFMS's official documents list common causes: Aadhaar not in the NPCI mapper, closed or inactive account, pending KYC, wrong IFSC or account number. Portal options and rules can change, so confirm with your bank or the scheme portal. How to check Aadhaar-bank seeding is covered in our separate guide.

Step by step

  1. First get your passbook updated or check mobile banking/SMS. Remember the money may have gone to another of your accounts: per the PFMS FAQ only one account can be seeded to an Aadhaar at a time and a fresh seeding overwrites the earlier one, so look at all your accounts.
  2. Open your scheme's official portal and look for 'Beneficiary Status' or 'Payment Status' (for example the PM-KISAN portal) to see whether the application is approved or the payment released.
  3. A secondary source says the 'Know Your Payments' option on pfms.nic.in shows payments by bank account number, Aadhaar or token number. We could not open pfms.nic.in ourselves, so go by what the screen shows; it should need no login or fee.
  4. Read the status: if it is still 'sanctioned' or in process, the delay may be with the scheme department and you need to wait. If it shows rejected/failed, note the reason.
  5. Fix according to the reason. PFMS's document says for Aadhaar not seeded/mapped in NPCI, get Aadhaar seeded at the bank and ask the bank to update the NPCI mapper. For a closed or blocked account, wrong account number or wrong IFSC, give the scheme department a valid, active account.
  6. If the account has been inactive for long or KYC is pending, PFMS's remedy is to visit the bank branch and update KYC. If your name differs between Aadhaar and bank records, ask the branch to correct it; we found no separate official remedy for name mismatch.
  7. Give a written complaint to your bank branch first, then escalate to the bank's customer service cell and nodal officer. NPCI's FAQ describes this order and says if Aadhaar is not reflecting in the mapper, the bank is responsible for fixing it.
  8. For scheme-specific issues, approach the scheme's official portal/helpline and the district or block office. Take helpline numbers only from the scheme's official website.
  9. If this does not work, lodge a complaint on CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in) under 'Lodge Public Grievance'. Per the portal it is available 24x7, free of charge, and grievances sent by email are not attended to. Use the registration number in 'View Status'. If you are unhappy with the closure, give feedback; a 'Poor' rating enables an appeal.

Documents needed

  • Aadhaar number and bank account number (enter only on official portals or at your bank)
  • Bank passbook/statement showing IFSC and branch
  • Scheme application/registration number and date of last payment
  • Registered mobile number (for OTP/SMS)
  • Copy or receipt of your complaint and the CPGRAMS registration number

Keep in mind

  • Before concluding the money has not come, check all your bank accounts and passbooks; it may have gone to a recently seeded different account.
  • Never share Aadhaar, account details or OTP with unknown callers, links or agents. Use only government (.gov.in) portals.
  • Note the date, name and receipt/reference number of every complaint; it makes escalation easier.
  • We found no official timeline for resolution, so ask your bank or scheme portal and complain without delay.
  • No helpline numbers are given here on purpose; always take them from the scheme's or bank's official site.
