🍚 Ration Card e-KYC and Adding a Name

📤 Share:

Ration cards are run by the state food department. The e-KYC and add-name process differs by state; the surest route is your state's food portal or ration dealer.

Photo: Wietse Jongsma / Unsplash

Step by step

  1. Check e-KYC status on your state's food & supplies portal, the 'Mera Ration' app or at the ration shop (options differ by state).
  2. For e-KYC there is usually biometric Aadhaar on the shop's ePoS machine, or Aadhaar OTP/face on the portal/app.
  3. To add a name, apply for 'add member' on the state portal or at the food office.
  4. Attach the child's birth certificate, or marriage certificate plus proof of deletion from the old ration card (for a daughter-in-law), and all members' Aadhaar.
  5. After verification, download/receive the updated ration card.

Documents needed

  • Aadhaar of all members
  • Existing ration card
  • Birth/marriage certificate (as applicable)
  • Address proof

Keep in mind

  • Exemptions or separate rules for children's e-KYC may exist by state – ask the department.
  • If you live in another state, ask your home state's department about e-KYC options.
  • Skipping e-KYC can stop your ration, so do it on time.

Official / source links

  • National Food Security Portal (NFSA)
  • Mera Ration (Government of India)
Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
