⚠️ This is only an estimate. It assumes a 1 kW system makes about 120 units a month, which changes with season and location. Subsidy amounts and conditions can change by MNRE order. Confirm at pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

Subsidy calculation (as per reports)

  • ₹30,000 subsidy for 1 kW and ₹60,000 for 2 kW.
  • Maximum ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more.
  • Some states may give their own extra subsidy. It is not added here.
  • The subsidy reaches the bank account after verification. Timing can vary.
