📍 Government schemes of Uttar Pradesh

The schemes below are only for residents of this state. Central Government schemes apply in every state.

👧UP · Daughter

Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana (Uttar Pradesh)

Total ₹25,000 support for Uttar Pradesh daughters in six stages, from birth to graduation.

Uttar Pradesh Pension Scheme – Old Age, Widow & Disability (SSPY)
