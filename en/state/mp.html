📍 Government schemes of Madhya Pradesh

The schemes below are only for residents of this state. Central Government schemes apply in every state.

👩MP · Women

Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

₹1,500 a month for eligible women of Madhya Pradesh. New registrations are reported to be closed for now.

Full details →🎀MP · Daughter

Ladli Laxmi Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

Stage-wise financial help for girls from birth, through schooling, up to age 21.

Full details →🌾MP · Farmers

Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

₹4,000 a year extra from the state for farmers registered under PM Kisan.

Full details →👴MP · Pension

Madhya Pradesh Samagra Samajik Suraksha Pension Yojana

₹600 a month pension for eligible elderly, widows, abandoned women and disabled persons in Madhya Pradesh.

Full details →💍MP · Marriage

Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

Financial help to the bride when marrying at a government-organised mass wedding.

Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

Margin money and interest subsidy on bank loans for 18 to 40-year-olds starting a business.
