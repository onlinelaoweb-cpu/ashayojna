Mukhyamantri Amrutam (MA) Yojana / PMJAY-MA (Gujarat)

🏥 📍 Gujarat · Health & Family Welfare Department, Gujarat · Last checked: 12 November 2026

CoverCashless treatment up to ₹10 lakh
StartCover raised from ₹5 lakh from 11 July 2023
TreatmentsAbout 2,471 types of surgery/treatment
CardAyushman card
⚠️ Exact family eligibility and figures are based on 2023 news. Confirm the latest rules with the official portal or health department.

What is the scheme?

PMJAY-MA is Gujarat's health insurance scheme. In July 2023 its cover was raised from ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh per family per year. Complex treatments such as kidney, heart and liver transplants and cochlear implants are included.

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment up to ₹10 lakh
  • About 2,471 types of treatment and surgery (per reports), in government and private hospitals
  • Kidney, heart, liver transplants and cochlear implants included

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Eligible families of Gujarat holding an Ayushman (PMJAY-MA) card
  • The exact income/category conditions could not be fully verified here, check the official portal

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those without a card
  • Hospitals or treatments outside the scheme list

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Ayushman card
  • ID proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Ask at a nearby government hospital, CHC/PHC or gram panchayat about Ayushman card eligibility
  2. Get the card made
  3. Show the card and ID at a listed hospital
  4. Cashless treatment follows after pre-authorisation

Frequently asked questions

How much cover does MA give?

Cashless treatment up to ₹10 lakh.

How do I get the card?

Ask a nearby government hospital or the health department about eligibility and the process.

Can I get treatment in a private hospital?

Yes, at listed private hospitals too.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
