Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM)

👷 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Labour & Employment · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Pension₹3,000 a month (after age 60)
Entry age18 to 40 years
Monthly income₹15,000 or less
Government contributionMatching amount
⚠️ The age-wise contribution amounts could not be checked here. Confirm with a CSC or the official portal before enrolling.

What is the scheme?

PM-SYM is a pension scheme for unorganised sector workers. The member pays a fixed monthly amount and the Central Government adds an equal amount. After age 60 the member gets at least ₹3,000 a month as pension.

Key benefits

  • At least ₹3,000 a month pension after age 60
  • 50% of the pension as family pension to the spouse if the member dies
  • Matching contribution from the government

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Unorganised sector worker
  • Age 18 to 40 at entry
  • Monthly income ₹15,000 or less
  • A savings or Jan Dhan bank account

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Members of EPF, NPS or ESIC
  • Income tax payers

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Savings or Jan Dhan account number and IFSC

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Visit the nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) with Aadhaar and your bank/Jan Dhan account
  2. Enrolment is done on a self-certification basis
  3. Pay the first contribution in cash; later contributions are auto-debited from the next month
  4. Later you can also self-register on the PM-SYM web portal/mobile app
labour.gov.in – PM-SYM ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much contribution is to be paid?

It is fixed by age. The age-wise chart could not be checked here; see the CSC or the portal.

When does the pension begin?

After age 60.

Can EPF members join?

No, EPF, NPS or ESIC members are not eligible.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
