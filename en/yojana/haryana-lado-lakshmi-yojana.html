Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana (Haryana)

👩 📍 Haryana · Women & Child Development Department, Haryana · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly amount₹2,100
Split₹1,100 to account + ₹1,000 into a 5-year deposit
Age23 years or above
Income limit₹1 lakh a year (₹1.80 lakh in some cases)
⚠️ Based on a January 2026 news report. The application portal, full conditions and payment status could not be checked here; confirm with the official announcement.

What is the scheme?

Lado Lakshmi Yojana is a Haryana Government scheme that began on 25 September 2025. As per a January 2026 report the ₹2,100 is split in two: ₹1,100 to the beneficiary's account and ₹1,000 into a 5-year RD/FD in her name.

Key benefits

  • ₹2,100 a month (as per a January 2026 report)
  • ₹1,100 straight to the bank account and ₹1,000 into a government-managed 5-year deposit
  • If the beneficiary dies, the deposit with interest goes to her nominated heir

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Woman of Haryana, aged 23 or above
  • Annual family income originally up to ₹1 lakh
  • Mothers of children who scored above 80% in board exams at government schools can qualify with family income up to ₹1.80 lakh (per reports)
  • Mothers of children rehabilitated from severe or moderate malnutrition are also eligible (verified by Women & Child Development)

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those above the income limit
  • Those who do not meet other conditions (confirm the full conditions with the department)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Haryana residence proof
  • Proof of family income
  • Bank account details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check the registration process and portal from the Haryana Government's official announcements
  2. Give the documents asked for at registration/application
  3. After verification the money goes to the account and the deposit
