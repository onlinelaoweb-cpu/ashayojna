Kalibai Bheel Medhavi Chhatra Scooty Yojana (Rajasthan)

🛵 📍 Rajasthan · College Education Department, Rajasthan · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Assistance₹70,000 one-time (per one source)
Class 12 marksRBSE 65% or CBSE 75% (minimum)
Family incomeBelow ₹2.5 lakh
Applyhte.rajasthan.gov.in
⚠️ Sources disagreed on whether a scooty or cash is given. Confirm the amount and last date with the helpline or portal.

What is the scheme?

Under the Kalibai Bheel Medhavi Chhatra Scooty Yojana, meritorious college girls of Rajasthan get financial help to buy a scooty. Application is online through the SSO portal.

Key benefits

  • Per one source, ₹70,000 one-time help to an Aadhaar-linked bank account (DBT)
  • A ₹40,000 rule is mentioned for girls who earlier took the Class 10-based scooty

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Passed Class 12 last academic year with at least 65% (RBSE) or 75% (CBSE)
  • Regular undergraduate girl student in a Rajasthan college
  • Permanent resident of Rajasthan with annual family income below ₹2.5 lakh

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who already got a scooty under another state scheme (except the Class 10 exception)
  • Girls not studying regularly in college

Documents required

  • Jan Aadhaar card
  • Income certificate
  • Domicile certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet
  • Current-year college fee receipt
  • Bank details (in the girl's name, Jan Aadhaar-linked, DBT enabled)
  • Caste certificate (where applicable)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open 'Online Scholarship' on hte.rajasthan.gov.in
  2. Log in with SSO ID (sso.rajasthan.gov.in), register if you have none
  3. Choose the scheme, fill the form and upload documents
  4. Save the application number and check merit list/status on the portal
hte.rajasthan.gov.in – official portal ↗

📞 Helpline: 0141-2706106

Frequently asked questions

How much is given?

Per one source ₹70,000 one-time. Check the latest amount on the portal.

What marks are needed?

At least 65% in Class 12 (RBSE) or 75% (CBSE).

What is the last date?

One source says around 30 November 2026, but this is not officially confirmed. Check the portal.
