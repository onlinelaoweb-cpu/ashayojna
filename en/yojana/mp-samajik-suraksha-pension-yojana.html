Madhya Pradesh Samagra Samajik Suraksha Pension Yojana

👴 📍 Madhya Pradesh · Social Justice & Empowerment Department, Madhya Pradesh · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Monthly pension₹600 (per one source)
CategoriesElderly (60+), widow, abandoned woman, disabled etc.
ApplyOffline: Gram Panchayat / Janpad Panchayat / Municipal Corporation
Portalsocialjustice.mp.gov.in
⚠️ Sources differed on the pension amount and disability eligibility. Confirm on socialjustice.mp.gov.in before applying.

What is the scheme?

Under the Samagra Samajik Suraksha Pension Yojana the Madhya Pradesh Government pays a monthly pension to eligible elderly, widows, abandoned women and disabled persons from poor families. Applications are open all year.

Key benefits

  • ₹600 a month pension (per one source)
  • Payment into the bank account
  • Applications can be made throughout the year

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Resident of Madhya Pradesh
  • Poor household (BPL card or proof of poverty)
  • Age 60 or above, or a category such as widow/abandoned woman/disabled
  • Not an income tax payer

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Income tax payers
  • Those already receiving another pension or not meeting the conditions

Documents required

  • Two passport photos
  • BPL card or destitute/poverty proof
  • Age proof
  • Disability certificate (if applicable)
  • Proof of widowhood/abandonment (if applicable)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Take the application form from socialjustice.mp.gov.in or get it from the Gram Panchayat/Janpad Panchayat/Municipal Corporation
  2. Fill the form and submit with documents
  3. After verification the pension is sanctioned
socialjustice.mp.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much pension does MP give?

One source gives ₹600 a month. Confirm the latest amount on the portal.

Where to apply?

At the Gram Panchayat or Janpad Panchayat (rural), municipal corporation/body (urban).

What is the age limit for disability pension?

Sources said different things, so confirm on the portal.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
