Rythu Bharosa (Telangana)

🌾 📍 Telangana · Agriculture Dept, Telangana · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Amount₹12,000 per acre a year
InstalmentsTwo seasons (Rabi/Kharif)
PaymentDBT
Started26 January (as per reports)
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Rythu Bharosa is the Telangana Government's farm investment support scheme in which eligible farmers get ₹12,000 per acre a year based on land. Reports mention discussion on limits and rules, so confirm the latest rules with the Agriculture Department.

Key benefits

  • ₹12,000 per acre a year
  • Money in two seasons directly to the account
  • Based on land records

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A farmer of Telangana
  • Valid land record (pattadar passbook)
  • A bank account linked to Aadhaar

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those whose land records are unclear
  • Those in an ineligible category as per the rules

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Pattadar passbook / land record
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Contact your Rythu Vedika/Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO)
  2. Check your name in the eligibility list
  3. If there is an error get it corrected with documents

Frequently asked questions

How much is paid?

₹12,000 per acre a year.

When is it paid?

In two seasons; the government announces the dates.
