Gruha Jyothi Yojana (Karnataka)

💡 📍 Karnataka · Energy Department, Karnataka · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Free electricity200 units per household
ForDomestic use only
StatusHouse-to-house survey planned (report)
ApplySeva Sindhu
⚠️ As per a July 2026 report the scheme is under review. The 200-unit limit or eligibility may change; confirm with Seva Sindhu or the electricity company.

What is the scheme?

Under Gruha Jyothi, domestic electricity consumers in Karnataka get up to 200 units a month free. A July 2026 report says the Energy Department found some commercial establishments also taking the benefit, and the government will run a house-to-house survey to identify deserving beneficiaries.

Key benefits

  • Up to 200 units of free electricity per household, for domestic use

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Domestic electricity consumers in Karnataka
  • Residential use only
  • See Seva Sindhu or your electricity company for exact conditions

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Commercial-use connections
  • Those found ineligible in the survey

Documents required

  • Electricity account number
  • Aadhaar card
  • Residence/tenancy proof (for tenants)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open the scheme's application on sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in
  2. Register with your electricity account number and Aadhaar
  3. Ask your electricity company about application status and any survey/re-verification notice
sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in – Seva Sindhu ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much electricity is free?

Up to 200 units per household.

Will a shop get it?

No, the scheme is only for residential use.

Is the scheme changing?

A report says a review and house-to-house survey are planned. Confirm the latest rules with your electricity company.
