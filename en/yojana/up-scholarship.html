Uttar Pradesh Scholarship (Pre-Matric & Post-Matric)

🎓 📍 Uttar Pradesh · Social Welfare / Backward Classes / Minority Welfare Departments, Uttar Pradesh · Last checked: 11 November 2026

For whomClass 9–12 and graduation/PG/diploma/ITI etc.
Income limitGeneral/OBC/Minority: ₹2.5 lakh · SC/ST: ₹2 lakh (as reported)
RegistrationOne-time registration (OTR)
Portalscholarship.up.gov.in
⚠️ Dates, income limits and amounts change every year. For this year's exact information check scholarship.up.gov.in.

What is the scheme?

The Uttar Pradesh Government gives scholarship and fee reimbursement to students of eligible categories (mainly OBC, SC, ST and minorities) from class 9 up to post-graduation. Applications are taken every year in a fixed window on scholarship.up.gov.in.

Key benefits

  • Scholarship/fee reimbursement by level of study and category (see amounts on the portal)
  • Application, verification and payment online
  • Both new and renewal applications

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
  • Student of a recognised school/college/university (class 9 to PG, diploma, ITI etc.)
  • Income limit: General/OBC/Minority up to ₹2.5 lakh, SC/ST up to ₹2 lakh (see the latest limit on the portal)
  • Eligible category: mainly OBC, SC, ST, minorities (rules for others on the portal)

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who do not apply by the portal's last date
  • Those whose income exceeds the limit
  • Applications with incomplete or wrong documents/bank details

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Income certificate
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Residence certificate
  • Previous class marksheet
  • Bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
  • School/college admission/fee receipt

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open scholarship.up.gov.in and register (OTR)
  2. Choose category (pre-matric/post-matric) and new or renewal
  3. Log in and fill personal, academic and bank details
  4. Upload documents, submit and download the confirmation page
  5. After verification by the school/college the amount comes to the account
scholarship.up.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

When is the last date to apply?

Dates differ every year and windows usually open till around October. Check the latest date on the portal.

Do I have to apply again every year?

Yes, a renewal application must be made every year.

What is the income limit?

As reported, ₹2.5 lakh for General/OBC/Minority and ₹2 lakh for SC/ST. Confirm the latest limit on the portal.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
