Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana (Rajasthan)

🌸 📍 Rajasthan · Women & Child Development Department, Rajasthan · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Total support₹50,000 (in six stages)
Birth-date conditionGirls born 1 June 2016 to 31 July 2024
Born on/after 1 Aug 2024Registered under Lado Protsahan Yojana
PaymentTo the guardian's Aadhaar-linked bank account
⚠️ Firm information on the income condition and application process could not be found. The amounts for Lado Protsahan for new births are also not here. Confirm with the department.

What is the scheme?

Rajshri is a Rajasthan Government scheme that gives a total of ₹50,000 in six stages from a girl's birth to passing class 12. Girls born on or after 1 August 2024 are now registered under the Lado Protsahan Yojana.

Key benefits

  • ₹2,500 on institutional birth
  • ₹2,500 at age 1 after full immunisation
  • ₹4,000 on admission to class 1
  • ₹5,000 on admission to class 6
  • ₹11,000 on admission to class 10
  • ₹25,000 on completing class 12

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Girl born between 1 June 2016 and 31 July 2024 (after that, Lado Protsahan Yojana)
  • For the first instalment the birth must be in a government facility or a private institution registered under Janani Suraksha Yojana
  • Mother is a Rajasthan resident linked to Jan Aadhaar
  • Benefit for the first two living girls (third daughter gets only the first two instalments)
  • After class 1 the girl must study in a government or state-recognised school

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • No first instalment in case of a home birth
  • Girls born after 1 August 2024 come under Lado Protsahan instead of Rajshri

Documents required

  • Girl's birth certificate
  • Jan Aadhaar card
  • Hospital delivery record
  • Immunisation card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank passbook
  • Residence proof
  • School admission proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Ask your Anganwadi centre or district Women & Child Development office about the scheme's status
  2. Register after the birth and immunisation
  3. After school admission the school will enter the girl's details on the Shala Darpan portal
  4. Each stage's amount comes directly to the bank account
wcd.rajasthan.gov.in – Women & Child Development Dept ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much does Rajshri give in total?

A total of ₹50,000 in six stages.

Will a newly born girl get it?

Girls born on or after 1 August 2024 are registered under Lado Protsahan Yojana. Its amount may change, so ask the Anganwadi or the department.

Whose account does the money go to?

The parent's/guardian's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
