Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

🛡️ 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Finance · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Cover₹2 lakh (death due to any cause)
Premium₹436 per year
Age18 to 50 years (cover till 55)
RenewalEvery year 1 June to 31 May
⚠️ The government may change the premium and terms. Confirm with your bank or jansuraksha.gov.in.

What is the scheme?

PMJJBY is a one-year renewable life insurance scheme. The premium is auto-debited from your savings account each year and on death from any cause the nominee receives ₹2 lakh.

Key benefits

  • ₹2 lakh life insurance
  • Low premium: ₹436 per year
  • Auto-debit from the bank account, no extra hassle

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Age 18 to 50 years
  • Has a bank/post office savings account
  • Aadhaar-linked account
  • Name your nominee when joining

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • New applicants above 50 years
  • If the account lacks balance for the premium, the cover can lapse

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Savings account details
  • Nominee details
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Find the PMJJBY option at your bank/post office branch, net banking or mobile app
  2. Fill the form and give consent for auto-debit
  3. Fill the nominee's name and relationship
  4. Keep enough balance so the premium can be debited every year
jansuraksha.gov.in – Jan Suraksha portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

What is the PMJJBY premium?

₹436 per year (the premium can change, so confirm with the bank).

How to make a claim?

The nominee must submit the claim form, death certificate and documents at the bank branch.

Is death due to any cause covered?

Yes, as per scheme terms ₹2 lakh is paid for death from any cause.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
