Gruha Jyothi – 200 Units Free Power (Telangana)

⚡ 📍 Telangana · Energy Dept, Government of Telangana (power DISCOMs) · Last checked: 15 November 2026

BenefitUp to 200 units free
ForDomestic connections
ConditionConsumption within 200 units
IDAadhaar and ration card (as per reports)
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Gruha Jyothi is a Telangana Government scheme in which an eligible domestic electricity consumer gets up to 200 units free every month. The government pays the power companies every month, so an eligible household gets a zero bill. As per reports the scheme is still running in 2026 and money has been sanctioned for 2026-27.

Key benefits

  • Up to 200 units free every month
  • Zero bill if use stays within 200 units
  • As per reports, tenants have also been said to be eligible

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A resident of Telangana
  • A domestic (home) connection, not a shop/commercial one
  • Aadhaar and ration card linked to the power connection (as per reports)
  • Electricity use within 200 units

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Commercial connections
  • As per reports, those with unpaid old electricity bills
  • Those whose use stays above the limit (ask the power company for the exact rule)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Electricity bill / connection number
  • For tenants, proof that the meter is in the landlord's name

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Get your power connection linked with Aadhaar and ration card
  2. Ask the power company meter reader/office or the Praja Palana centre (municipal/MPDO office)
  3. Fill the application and submit documents
  4. After verification the free units show in your bill
telangana.gov.in – Telangana Government main portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

What if use goes even a little above 200 units?

As per reports the benefit is lost if the limit is crossed, and some MLAs have complained about this. Ask the power company for the exact rule.

Can tenants get it?

The power company had said tenants are also eligible. Keep proof of the meter owner with you.

Is the application online?

As per reports the application mainly goes through Praja Palana/municipal or mandal offices and the power company. An online option is not confirmed.
