Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)

🏡 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Rural Development · Last checked: 18 November 2026

Assistance (plains)₹1,20,000
Hilly / North-East₹1,30,000
Toilet support₹12,000 (Swachh Bharat Mission-Gramin)
MGNREGA labour90 days (plains), 95 days (hilly/North-East)
⚠️ Sources checked (18 Nov 2026): Outlook Money (11 Aug 2025), Indian Pay Calculator (19 Jul 2026), ThePrint/PTI (10 Dec 2024). The ₹1.2 lakh/₹1.3 lakh amounts agree across all three. We did not read the pmayg.nic.in homepage; the helpline and latest exclusion rules (refrigerator, two-wheeler etc.) could not be confirmed, so re-check the older rules above with your panchayat.

What is the scheme?

PMAY-G aims to give pucca houses to eligible rural families living in kutcha houses or without a home. Beneficiaries are chosen mainly from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 list and the Awaas+ survey for left-out families. Assistance comes in three instalments directly to the bank account. As per Outlook Money (Aug 2025) the scheme has been extended till 31 March 2029 with a target of 2 crore more pucca houses. A July 2026 report mentions interim approval for 1 lakh new houses for West Bengal.

Key benefits

  • ₹1.20 lakh (plains) / ₹1.30 lakh (hilly and North-East states)
  • Three instalments: ₹25,000 on sanction, ₹45,000 at lintel level, ₹50,000 on roof completion
  • Separate ₹12,000 for a toilet
  • 90/95 days of unskilled labour under MGNREGA
  • As per Outlook Money (2025) the Centre-State cost share is 60:40 in plains states and 90:10 in North-East/Himalayan states and J&K-Ladakh
  • As per Outlook Money an extra loan of up to ₹70,000 with an interest subsidy (starting at 3%) may be available if needed; assistance is reported up to ₹1.8 lakh in Odisha

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Selection is mainly from the SECC 2011 list; left-out families can join via the Awaas+ 2024 survey
  • Priority: those in kutcha houses, homeless families, manual scavengers, primitive tribal groups, freed bonded labourers
  • At least 60% of the total target is for SC/ST families

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Family owns a motorised two/three/four-wheeler or mechanised farm equipment
  • KCC limit of ₹50,000 or more
  • Any member earning above ₹15,000 a month, a government employee, or paying income/professional tax
  • Family with a registered non-farm enterprise
  • More than 2.5 acres irrigated or 5 acres unirrigated land
  • Those who already have a house with pucca roof and walls

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • BPL card or MGNREGA job card
  • Land papers (if any)
  • Caste certificate (for SC/ST)
  • Mobile number and passport photo

How to apply (step by step)

  1. There is no direct online application. First ask at the Gram Panchayat or Block office (BDO) whether your name is on the list
  2. If not, get your name added through the Awaas+ 2024 survey; officials enter details on the AwaasSoft portal
  3. Or do a self-survey on the 'AwaasPlus 2024' mobile app with Aadhaar and face authentication and note the registration number
  4. After verification and state approval, if your name is on the list the house is sanctioned (the survey does not guarantee a house)
  5. Check status on pmayg.nic.in under 'Stakeholders' → 'IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary' with your registration number
  6. As per a July 2026 report the Awaas+ survey is done by self-survey on the AwaasApp or via the Gram Panchayat/Block office; check the list on pmayg.nic.in. In West Bengal the survey deadline was moved from 20 July to 15 August 2026 because of rain (Bengal only); ask your panchayat for your state's date

How to check your status

  1. Open the beneficiary search option on the official PMAY-G portal and look up your details by registration number or Aadhaar/name (the site menu may change).
  2. See the instalment, geo-tagging and inspection status.
  3. You can also ask at the gram panchayat or block office (BDO).

Payment not received? Possible reasons

  • As per reports, the next instalment can be held if the geo-tagged photo of a construction stage is not uploaded. Ask the panchayat secretary or housing assistant.
  • The name is not on the Awaas Plus/eligible list.
  • Aadhaar is not linked to the bank account (NPCI mapping). Ask the bank branch, or check 'Bank Seeding Status' at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in (the menu name may change).
  • e-KYC / verification is pending. Complete it online, at a CSC or at the concerned office.
  • Name or date of birth is different in the application and in Aadhaar/bank records.
  • The bank account is closed or dormant, or the account number/IFSC on the portal is wrong.
  • The instalment was released late because of government approval, funds or an election code of conduct. In that case payments are held for everyone in the state, not only for you.

Frequently asked questions

How much money does PMAY-G give?

₹1.20 lakh in plains and ₹1.30 lakh in hilly/North-East states, in three instalments, plus ₹12,000 for a toilet.

Does applying cost money?

No. There is no charge to apply or to get your name added. Anyone asking for money is cheating; complain to the Gram Panchayat/BDO.

How to check my status?

On pmayg.nic.in search the beneficiary by registration number, or choose state-district-block-panchayat and search by name/Aadhaar.

Till when will the scheme run?

Till 31 March 2029 as per Outlook Money (Aug 2025). ThePrint/PTI (Dec 2024) had said 'five more years' and 2 crore houses.

Are states fined for delays?

As per Outlook Money there is a per-house, per-month penalty on states for delays. This applies to state governments, not to beneficiaries.

How to check your status

1. Open the beneficiary search option on the official PMAY-G portal and look up your details by registration number or Aadhaar/name (the site menu may change). 2. See the instalment, geo-tagging and inspection status. 3. You can also ask at the gram panchayat or block office (BDO).

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
