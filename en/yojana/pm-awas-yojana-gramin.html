Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)

🏡 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Rural Development · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Assistance (plains)₹1,20,000
Hilly / North-East₹1,30,000
Toilet support₹12,000 (Swachh Bharat Mission-Gramin)
MGNREGA labour90 days (plains), 95 days (hilly/North-East)
⚠️ Amounts and timing of instalments may vary slightly by state. See the latest eligibility conditions on pmayg.nic.in.

What is the scheme?

PMAY-G aims to give pucca houses to eligible rural families living in kutcha houses or without a home. Beneficiaries are chosen mainly from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 list and the Awaas+ survey for left-out families. Assistance comes in three instalments directly to the bank account.

Key benefits

  • ₹1.20 lakh (plains) / ₹1.30 lakh (hilly and North-East states)
  • Three instalments: ₹25,000 on sanction, ₹45,000 at lintel level, ₹50,000 on roof completion
  • Separate ₹12,000 for a toilet
  • 90/95 days of unskilled labour under MGNREGA

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Selection is mainly from the SECC 2011 list; left-out families can join via the Awaas+ 2024 survey
  • Priority: those in kutcha houses, homeless families, manual scavengers, primitive tribal groups, freed bonded labourers
  • At least 60% of the total target is for SC/ST families

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Family owns a motorised two/three/four-wheeler or mechanised farm equipment
  • KCC limit of ₹50,000 or more
  • Any member earning above ₹15,000 a month, a government employee, or paying income/professional tax
  • Family with a registered non-farm enterprise
  • More than 2.5 acres irrigated or 5 acres unirrigated land
  • Those who already have a house with pucca roof and walls

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • BPL card or MGNREGA job card
  • Land papers (if any)
  • Caste certificate (for SC/ST)
  • Mobile number and passport photo

How to apply (step by step)

  1. There is no direct online application. First ask at the Gram Panchayat or Block office (BDO) whether your name is on the list
  2. If not, get your name added through the Awaas+ 2024 survey; officials enter details on the AwaasSoft portal
  3. Or do a self-survey on the 'AwaasPlus 2024' mobile app with Aadhaar and face authentication and note the registration number
  4. After verification and state approval, if your name is on the list the house is sanctioned (the survey does not guarantee a house)
  5. Check status on pmayg.nic.in under 'Stakeholders' → 'IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary' with your registration number
pmayg.nic.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much money does PMAY-G give?

₹1.20 lakh in plains and ₹1.30 lakh in hilly/North-East states, in three instalments, plus ₹12,000 for a toilet.

Does applying cost money?

No. There is no charge to apply or to get your name added. Anyone asking for money is cheating; complain to the Gram Panchayat/BDO.

How to check my status?

On pmayg.nic.in search the beneficiary by registration number, or choose state-district-block-panchayat and search by name/Aadhaar.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
