Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

🔥 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Subsidy₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder (DBT)
Subsidised refillsUp to 9 a year in FY 2025-26 (Cabinet, Aug 2025)
ApplicantWoman aged 18+
Official portalpmuy.gov.in
⚠️ Sources checked (17 Nov 2026): the PMO page (8 Aug 2025), Free Press Journal (10 Aug 2025), the pmuy.gov.in homepage and FAQ. No new 2026-27 subsidy approval or refill cut could be confirmed. Confirm on pmuy.gov.in or with your gas agency before applying.

What is the scheme?

Ujjwala aims to give women in poor families an LPG connection and relief from wood and dung-cake smoke. The connection is in the eligible woman's name and a targeted subsidy on refills comes straight to the bank account.

Key benefits

  • LPG connection for eligible families
  • ₹300 subsidy on a 14.2 kg cylinder, via DBT to the bank account
  • As per the Cabinet's August 2025 decision (PMO page) the ₹300 subsidy covers up to 9 refills a year in FY 2025-26, with about ₹12,000 crore approved. One 2026 website claims a cut to 4 refills, but this is not confirmed by government pages or major newspapers, and we found no FY 2026-27 approval. Ask your gas agency for the latest rule
  • First refill and stove free (per the PMO page and the pmuy.gov.in FAQ)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Applicant is a woman aged 18 or above
  • Family is poor/eligible category and has no LPG connection at home
  • Eligible categories: the pmuy.gov.in FAQ lists SECC households, SC/ST, PMAY-G, Antyodaya (AAY) etc., while the homepage speaks of a deprivation declaration. Confirm with your gas distributor
  • Valid Aadhaar and bank account required

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who already have a regular domestic LPG connection
  • Families outside the eligible categories
  • Those whose Aadhaar, bank account or LPG details are not linked for DBT

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Ration card / proof of family eligibility
  • Passport-size photo
  • KYC form (available at the gas agency)
  • Aadhaar identity and biometric Aadhaar authentication (done by the distributor, per the pmuy.gov.in FAQ)
  • Family deprivation declaration (mentioned on the pmuy.gov.in homepage)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check the application option on pmuy.gov.in, or visit the nearest LPG distributor (HP / Indane / Bharat Gas)
  2. Submit the filled KYC application and required documents
  3. After eligibility check the connection is issued
  4. On taking refills, the subsidy comes to the bank account as per eligibility

How to check your status

  1. Ask your gas distributor (agency) whether the subsidy was paid.
  2. Log in at mylpg.in with the LPG ID/mobile to see the cylinder and subsidy history (the site menu may change).
  3. Look for a 'DBT/LPG' entry in the bank passbook.

Payment not received? Possible reasons

  • As per reports, if biometric e-KYC is pending at the distributor, subsidy or the connection can be affected. Ask the distributor.
  • The bank account on the LPG connection is wrong or not linked with Aadhaar.
  • Aadhaar is not linked to the bank account (NPCI mapping). Ask the bank branch, or check 'Bank Seeding Status' at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in (the menu name may change).
  • e-KYC / verification is pending. Complete it online, at a CSC or at the concerned office.
  • Name or date of birth is different in the application and in Aadhaar/bank records.
  • The bank account is closed or dormant, or the account number/IFSC on the portal is wrong.
  • The instalment was released late because of government approval, funds or an election code of conduct. In that case payments are held for everyone in the state, not only for you.

Frequently asked questions

How much subsidy does Ujjwala give?

₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder, for up to 9 refills a year in FY 2025-26 (Cabinet, Aug 2025). A report of a cut to 4 refills in 2026 is not officially confirmed; ask your gas agency or pmuy.gov.in for the latest rule.

Can men apply?

No, the connection is given only in the name of an adult woman of the family.

What if the subsidy is not coming?

Aadhaar, bank account and LPG connection must be linked (DBT). Get it checked with your gas distributor.

How to check your status

1. Ask your gas distributor (agency) whether the subsidy was paid. 2. Log in at mylpg.in with the LPG ID/mobile to see the cylinder and subsidy history (the site menu may change). 3. Look for a 'DBT/LPG' entry in the bank passbook.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
