Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

🔥 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas · Last checked: 11 November 2026

Subsidy₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder (DBT)
Subsidised refillsUp to 4 a year (per June 2026 reports)
ApplicantWoman aged 18+
Official portalpmuy.gov.in
⚠️ Ujjwala subsidy rules changed recently and may change again. This information is based on media reports, so confirm with pmuy.gov.in or your gas agency before applying.

What is the scheme?

Ujjwala aims to give women in poor families an LPG connection and relief from wood and dung-cake smoke. The connection is in the eligible woman's name and a targeted subsidy on refills comes straight to the bank account.

Key benefits

  • LPG connection for eligible families
  • ₹300 subsidy on a 14.2 kg cylinder, via DBT to the bank account
  • Per June 2026 media reports, subsidised refills were cut from 9 to 4 a year (up to ₹1,200 annually). Later cylinders are at market price

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Applicant is a woman aged 18 or above
  • Family is poor/eligible category and has no LPG connection at home
  • SECC 2011 households, SC/ST families, PMAY-G and Antyodaya (AAY) beneficiaries, most backward classes, forest dwellers, tea garden tribes, islanders and poor families notified by Centre/State
  • Valid Aadhaar and bank account required

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who already have a regular domestic LPG connection
  • Families outside the eligible categories
  • Those whose Aadhaar, bank account or LPG details are not linked for DBT

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Ration card / proof of family eligibility
  • Passport-size photo
  • KYC form (available at the gas agency)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check the application option on pmuy.gov.in, or visit the nearest LPG distributor (HP / Indane / Bharat Gas)
  2. Submit the filled KYC application and required documents
  3. After eligibility check the connection is issued
  4. On taking refills, the subsidy comes to the bank account as per eligibility
pmuy.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much subsidy does Ujjwala give?

₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder. As per June 2026 reports it applies to 4 refills a year. Confirm the latest rules with your gas agency or pmuy.gov.in.

Can men apply?

No, the connection is given only in the name of an adult woman of the family.

What if the subsidy is not coming?

Aadhaar, bank account and LPG connection must be linked (DBT). Get it checked with your gas distributor.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
