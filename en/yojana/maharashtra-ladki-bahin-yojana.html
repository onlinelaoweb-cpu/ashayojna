Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana (Maharashtra)

👩 📍 Maharashtra · Women & Child Development Department, Maharashtra · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Monthly amount₹1,500
e-KYCFinal deadline was 30 April 2026
BeneficiariesAbout 1.88 crore completed the process (report)
Portalladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in
⚠️ Beneficiary numbers and payment dates come from news figures of different times. Confirm exact eligibility and the latest status on the official portal.

What is the scheme?

Under the Ladki Bahin Yojana the Maharashtra Government sends ₹1,500 a month to eligible women's bank accounts. e-KYC was made mandatory and reports said about 68 lakh accounts were closed after the deadline.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,500 a month directly in the bank account (DBT)
  • The state releases about ₹3,700 crore a month (May 2026 report)
  • The state decided not to recover money already paid to ineligible beneficiaries (per reports)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Woman resident of Maharashtra
  • Must have completed e-KYC
  • No member of the family is a government employee
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • See the portal for exact income and age conditions

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who did not complete e-KYC by the deadline (their accounts were reported closed)
  • Families with a government employee
  • Those who do not meet other conditions

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Residence/domicile proof
  • Income information (where asked)
  • Mobile number (for e-KYC OTP)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in
  2. Check your application and e-KYC status
  3. If you had e-KYC trouble, contact your Anganwadi or district office
  4. See payment status on the portal/bank passbook
ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much does Ladki Bahin give now?

₹1,500 a month.

What if e-KYC was not done?

Reports say accounts without e-KYC were closed. Confirm your status on the portal or district office.

Can an instalment get stuck?

Reports mention some beneficiaries' instalments were held during verification, so check the portal.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
