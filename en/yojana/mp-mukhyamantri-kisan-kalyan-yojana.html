Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

🌾 📍 Madhya Pradesh · Farmer Welfare & Agriculture Development Department, Madhya Pradesh · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Yearly amount₹4,000
InstalmentsTwo of ₹2,000
ConditionMust be registered under PM Kisan
Total (with PM Kisan)₹6,000 + ₹4,000 = ₹10,000 a year
⚠️ Instalment dates and rules depend on government announcements. The information here is based on news sources, so confirm with the agriculture department/Patwari.

What is the scheme?

Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana is a Madhya Pradesh Government scheme that gives an extra ₹4,000 a year from the state on top of PM Kisan Samman Nidhi. Only farmers registered under PM Kisan can take the benefit.

Key benefits

  • ₹4,000 a year in two instalments of ₹2,000 into the bank account (DBT)
  • Together with PM Kisan's ₹6,000, a total of ₹10,000 a year

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh and an active farmer owning cultivable land
  • Registered under PM Kisan Samman Nidhi
  • Application is approved after verification by the Patwari

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Holders of constitutional posts, MPs/MLAs, mayors
  • Most government employees (except Class IV) and those with a monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more
  • Recent income tax payers
  • Professionals such as doctors and lawyers

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land papers (khasra/khatauni)
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • PM Kisan registration number
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. First make sure you are registered under PM Kisan and e-KYC is done
  2. Ask your Patwari/gram panchayat or agriculture department about the scheme's application/verification
  3. On approval you receive confirmation
  4. See instalment status and dates as per government announcements

Frequently asked questions

How much money does this scheme give?

₹4,000 a year in two instalments. It is in addition to PM Kisan's ₹6,000.

Is it necessary to be on PM Kisan?

Yes, only farmers registered under PM Kisan get the benefit.

When will the instalment come?

Instalment dates depend on government announcements, so no date is given here.
