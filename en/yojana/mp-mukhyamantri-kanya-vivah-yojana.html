Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

💍 📍 Madhya Pradesh · Social Justice & Empowerment Department, Madhya Pradesh · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Assistance₹49,000 cheque to the bride (per one source)
ConditionMarriage at a government mass wedding
AgeBride 18+, groom 21+
Portalvivahportal.mp.gov.in
⚠️ This is based on a private guide. Confirm the amount and rules on vivahportal.mp.gov.in.

What is the scheme?

Under the Kanya Vivah Yojana the Madhya Pradesh Government helps poor families with their daughters' weddings. The benefit is given only when the marriage takes place at a mass wedding organised by the government.

Key benefits

  • ₹49,000 account payee cheque to the bride on the wedding day, as per one source
  • The mass wedding ceremony is arranged by the government/local body
  • No application fee

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Bride at least 18 and groom at least 21 years old
  • Bride or her guardian is a permanent resident of Madhya Pradesh
  • Open to General, OBC, SC and ST (one source mentions no income limit)
  • Marriage takes place at a government-organised mass wedding

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Individual weddings held privately
  • Those who do not meet the age condition

Documents required

  • Age proof of bride and groom (birth certificate, Aadhaar or school certificate)
  • Madhya Pradesh residence proof of bride or guardian
  • Samagra ID of bride and guardian
  • Bank passbook in the bride's name
  • Passport-size photograph

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Find the next mass wedding from your gram panchayat/urban body, vivahportal.mp.gov.in or CM Helpline 181
  2. Apply to the CEO of the Janpad Panchayat (rural) or the Municipal Commissioner/officer (urban)
  3. A decision follows verification (within 15 working days, per one source)
  4. The cheque is given on the day of the ceremony
vivahportal.mp.gov.in – marriage portal ↗

📞 CM Helpline: 181

Frequently asked questions

How much is given?

Per one source, a ₹49,000 cheque to the bride. The amount may change, so check the latest on the portal.

Is there a benefit for a private wedding?

No, the benefit is only for weddings at a government mass wedding.

Where to apply?

At the Janpad Panchayat in rural areas and the municipal office in urban areas.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
