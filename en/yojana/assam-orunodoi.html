Orunodoi Scheme (Assam)

👩 📍 Assam · Finance Dept, Government of Assam · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly amountReported at ₹1,250 or more recently
WhoWoman head of the family
PaymentDBT, direct to bank account
Portalorunodoi.assam.gov.in
⚠️ There are reports of schemes and amounts changing in Assam after 2026. Do not treat the amount here as final; confirm on the official portal.
Advertisement

What is the scheme?

Orunodoi is an Assam Government DBT scheme that gives monthly financial aid to the woman head of selected needy families. The amount was raised recently and may change again because of the new government's promises.

Key benefits

  • Monthly financial aid directly in the bank account
  • Amount reported above ₹1,250 recently; reports mention a separate arrangement for flood-hit districts
  • Payment without middlemen

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A permanent woman resident of Assam, head of the family
  • Family income within the fixed limit
  • A bank account linked to Aadhaar

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Families with a government job or higher income
  • Those in other excluded categories

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Watch for the announcement of the latest application round and see orunodoi.assam.gov.in
  2. Fill the application and give the documents
  3. After verification your name comes in the list

Frequently asked questions

How much is paid?

Reported at ₹1,250 or more recently. It may change with the new government's announcements; confirm on the portal.

Are applications always open?

No, the government opens them in rounds.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
