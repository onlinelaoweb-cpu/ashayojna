Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana (Punjab)

🏥 📍 Punjab · State Health Agency, Punjab · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Cover₹10 lakh a year (family)
TreatmentCashless
CardSehat card
Portalsha.punjab.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

This is a Punjab Government health scheme giving families cashless treatment at listed hospitals. As per reports there is no age, gender or income barrier for the Sehat card, but documents such as Aadhaar and voter ID are needed.

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment up to ₹10 lakh a year per family
  • Available at listed government and private hospitals
  • Reports of many treatments including for newborns

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A resident family of Punjab
  • Aadhaar and voter ID (as per reports)
  • Registration for the Sehat card

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who are not residents of Punjab
  • Those without the required ID documents

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Voter ID / residence proof
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to the nearest Sehat card registration centre/camp or sha.punjab.gov.in
  2. Register with Aadhaar and ID documents
  3. After the card is made take treatment at a listed hospital
sha.punjab.gov.in – State Health Agency ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much cover is there?

Up to ₹10 lakh a year cashless.

What is needed for the card?
