Delhi Old Age Pension Scheme

👵 📍 Delhi · Social Welfare Department, Delhi Government · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Ages 60-69₹2,000 a month
Age 70+₹2,500 a month
SC/ST/minorityExtra ₹500
Applyedistrict.delhigovt.nic.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules are based on an April 2025 report and may have changed. Confirm on the e-District portal whether applications are open.

What is the scheme?

Under the Delhi Government's old age pension eligible senior citizens get a monthly pension directly in their bank account. Application is online on the e-District portal.

Key benefits

  • ₹2,000 a month for ages 60 to 69
  • ₹2,500 a month for age 70 or more
  • Extra ₹500 a month for SC, ST and minority applicants (per an April 2025 report)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Age 60 or more
  • At least 5 years of residence in Delhi before applying
  • Annual family income from all sources below ₹1 lakh
  • A solely operated, Aadhaar-linked Delhi bank account
  • Not receiving any other government pension/assistance

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those already getting another pension or government financial help
  • Those above the income limit
  • Those whose bank account is not Aadhaar-linked

Documents required

  • Age proof (birth certificate, Aadhaar or voter ID)
  • Proof of 5 years' Delhi residence (ration card or bills)
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account details
  • Income self-declaration
  • Caste certificate for SC/ST (ages 60-69)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in and log in/register
  2. Fill the old age pension application
  3. Upload the required documents
  4. After verification the pension comes to the bank account
edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in – e-District portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much old age pension does Delhi give?

₹2,000 for ages 60-69 and ₹2,500 for 70+, with ₹500 extra for SC/ST/minorities (April 2025 report).

Are applications open now?

One report says applications open for a limited number or period, so check the latest on the portal.

What is the income limit?

Annual family income below ₹1 lakh.
