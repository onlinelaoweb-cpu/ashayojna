300 Units Free Electricity (Punjab)

💡 📍 Punjab · PSPCL / Government of Punjab · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Free units300 a month (domestic)
WhoDomestic electricity consumers
ApplicationNo separate application, applied in the bill
BodyPSPCL
⚠️ Power tariffs and rules keep changing. This is based on reports; confirm with PSPCL.

What is the scheme?

The Punjab Government gives domestic consumers up to 300 units of electricity free every month. As per reports most households now get zero bills, and a recent cut in power tariffs has also been reported.

Key benefits

  • Up to 300 units of electricity free every month
  • The relief comes automatically in the bill
  • No separate application reported to be needed

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A domestic electricity consumer in Punjab
  • A valid, working meter connection
  • Consumption within the 300-unit rule

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Commercial/industrial connections
  • Those whose consumption is outside the rule's limit

Documents required

  • Electricity bill / consumer account number
  • Aadhaar card
  • Address proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check your electricity bill and read the relief line
  2. If there is a problem go to the nearest PSPCL office
  3. Read the rules on the official website
pspcl.in – PSPCL website ↗

Frequently asked questions

Is a separate application needed?

Normally no, but confirm the rules with PSPCL.

What if consumption is above 300 units?

The conditions are in PSPCL's rules; ask them.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
