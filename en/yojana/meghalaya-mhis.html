Meghalaya Health Insurance Scheme (MHIS)

🏥 📍 Meghalaya · Health Dept, Meghalaya · Last checked: 15 November 2026

CoverUp to ₹5 lakh a year per family (as per reports)
TreatmentCashless
Central linkTogether with PM-JAY
Portalmeghealth.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

MHIS is the Meghalaya Government's health insurance scheme implemented together with Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY. Eligible families get cashless treatment at listed hospitals.

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment facility for the family
  • Available at listed government and private hospitals
  • Cover linked with Ayushman Bharat

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A resident family of Meghalaya
  • Falling in the fixed eligibility category
  • Aadhaar and identity documents

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who are not residents of the state
  • Those outside the eligibility category

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Residence proof
  • Family details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Ask about enrolment at the nearest health centre or hospital
  2. Enrol with the documents
  3. Take treatment at a listed hospital with the card
meghealth.gov.in – Health Dept ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much cover is there?

As per reports up to ₹5 lakh a year, but it can differ by eligibility.

Is Ayushman Bharat separate?

Both are run together.
