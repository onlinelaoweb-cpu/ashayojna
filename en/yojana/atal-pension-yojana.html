Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

👴 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Finance / PFRDA · Last checked: 18 November 2026

Monthly pension₹1,000 to ₹5,000 (after 60)
Age18 to 40 years
Contribution (example, ₹5,000 pension)₹210 at age 18, ₹577 at 30, ₹1,454 at 40 a month (Business Today, Apr 2026)
Bank accountSavings account required
⚠️ Sources checked (18 Nov 2026): Business Today (24 Apr 2026), Outlook Money (24 Apr and 21 May 2026). Age 18-40, ₹1,000-₹5,000 pension and the bar on income-tax payers agree across all three. Late-payment penalty rates, government co-contribution and account-closure rules were not found on these pages, so ask your bank. Note: about 9.1 crore subscribers by May 2026.

What is the scheme?

Atal Pension Yojana is for unorganised sector workers. You join between 18 and 40, pay a fixed monthly contribution based on your age and chosen pension, and after 60 receive ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month. As per Business Today (24 Apr 2026), APY crossed 9 crore gross enrolments by 21 April 2026, and over 1.35 crore new subscribers joined in FY 2025-26, the highest annual figure so far.

Key benefits

  • Fixed monthly pension after 60 (choose from ₹1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 4,000 or 5,000)
  • On the holder's death, the spouse gets the same pension, and after both the nominee gets the corpus
  • The younger you join, the lower the monthly contribution
  • Example monthly contribution for a ₹5,000 pension: ₹210 at age 18 (corpus of about ₹8.5 lakh), ₹577 at 30, ₹1,454 at 40 (Business Today, Apr 2026)
  • The pension slab can be changed once a year, typically in April (Business Today)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Age 18 to 40 years
  • Savings bank/post office account and Aadhaar
  • Valid mobile number

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Income taxpayers cannot make new applications from 1 October 2022
  • New applicants above 40 years

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Savings account
  • Mobile number
  • Nominee details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Take the APY form from your bank branch/post office, net banking or app
  2. Choose the pension amount you want (₹1,000 to ₹5,000)
  3. Consent to auto-debit from the bank account
  4. Add a nominee and make sure the monthly contribution keeps getting deposited

Frequently asked questions

How much will the monthly contribution be?

It depends on your age and chosen pension. Ask the bank for the APY chart to get the exact amount for your age.

Can income taxpayers join?

From 1 October 2022 income taxpayers cannot open new accounts.

What if I must stop contributing midway?

Penalty can apply for missed contributions and the account may be closed after a long gap. Ask the bank the rules.

Will the pension go above ₹5,000?

As per Outlook Money (21 May 2026) the Department of Financial Services is examining a pension above the ₹5,000 cap, but the PFRDA chairperson said it will take time and it is premature to conclude. Nothing has changed yet.

What happens if the subscriber dies?

If death is before 60 the spouse can continue contributions and claim the same pension (Business Today). Confirm the rules with your bank.

Is there an account fee?

As per Outlook Money (Apr 2026) account opening ₹15 and annual maintenance ₹15, no transaction charges. Confirm with your bank.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
