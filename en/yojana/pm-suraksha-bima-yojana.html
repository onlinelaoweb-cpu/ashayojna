Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

🩹 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Finance · Last checked: 11 November 2026

Death / total disability₹2 lakh
Partial disability₹1 lakh
Premium₹20 per year
Age18 to 70 years
⚠️ Sum insured, premium and conditions can change. See the latest information at your bank or jansuraksha.gov.in.

What is the scheme?

PMSBY is a one-year renewable accident insurance scheme. For a tiny premium it pays the sum insured on accidental death or disability.

Key benefits

  • ₹2 lakh on accidental death or total disability
  • ₹1 lakh on partial permanent disability
  • Very low premium: ₹20 per year

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Age 18 to 70 years
  • Has a bank/post office savings account
  • Aadhaar-linked account

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • People above 70 years
  • The policy can lapse if the account has no balance

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Savings account details
  • Nominee details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Choose PMSBY at the bank branch, net banking or app
  2. Fill the form and consent to auto-debit
  3. Enter the nominee's name
  4. Keep enough balance for the premium every year
jansuraksha.gov.in – Jan Suraksha portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

What premium does PMSBY charge?

₹20 per year (confirm with the bank as it may change).

What if I have more than one bank account?

Generally a person can be covered under this scheme through only one bank account.

When is the claim paid?

After an accident, the nominee/account holder must lodge the claim at the bank within the set period. Ask the bank the rules.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
