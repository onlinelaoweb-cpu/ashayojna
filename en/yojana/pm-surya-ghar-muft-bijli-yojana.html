PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

☀️ 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) · Last checked: 12 November 2026

1 kW₹30,000 subsidy
2 kW₹60,000 subsidy
3 kW or moreMaximum ₹78,000
Official portalpmsuryaghar.gov.in
⚠️ Subsidy amounts and conditions can change by MNRE order. Check pmsuryaghar.gov.in before applying.

What is the scheme?

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana the Central Government gives a subsidy for installing rooftop solar on your home. Making your own electricity cuts the bill, and you can sell surplus power to the grid (net metering).

Key benefits

  • Subsidy: about 60% of system cost up to 2 kW and 40% for the next 1 kW
  • Maximum subsidy ₹78,000 (3 kW system)
  • Big cut in electricity bill
  • Subsidy usually reaches the bank account about 15 days after verification (timing may vary)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Indian citizen
  • Own house with a roof suitable for solar
  • Valid electricity connection at home
  • Has not taken any earlier rooftop solar subsidy

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those without a roof suitable for solar
  • Those who have taken a similar subsidy before

Documents required

  • Recent electricity bill (consumer number)
  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account details / cancelled cheque
  • Mobile number and email
  • Roof details / photo (as needed)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in by choosing your state and electricity company (DISCOM)
  2. Log in with consumer number and apply for rooftop solar
  3. Wait for feasibility approval from the DISCOM
  4. Get the system installed by a registered vendor listed on the portal
  5. Apply for net metering and receive the subsidy after inspection
pmsuryaghar.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much subsidy does Surya Ghar give?

₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and a maximum of ₹78,000 for 3 kW or more.

Can I install it in a rented house?

Generally you must own the house and roof. Ask your DISCOM about the conditions.

How to choose a vendor?

Use only registered vendors listed on the portal and compare rates before starting work.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
