Mukhyamantri Nishchay Swayam Sahayata Bhatta Yojana (Bihar)

🧑‍🎓 📍 Bihar · Labour Resources Department / Saat Nishchay-2 programme, Bihar · Last checked: 11 November 2026

Allowance₹1,000 per month
DurationMaximum 2 years
Age20 to 25 years
QualificationGraduate (Arts/Science/Commerce)
⚠️ Eligibility and application conditions can change. The information here is based on news sources, so confirm on the portal.

What is the scheme?

Under the Mukhyamantri Nishchay Swayam Sahayata Bhatta Yojana, unemployed graduate youth of Bihar receive ₹1,000 a month for up to 2 years as financial support while they look for a job or self-employment. The scheme was earlier for Intermediate-pass youth and was later extended to graduates.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,000 per month, directly to the bank account
  • For a maximum of 2 years
  • Help connecting to skill training and job opportunities in this period (see portal)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Resident of Bihar
  • Age 20 to 25 years
  • Graduate (Arts/Science/Commerce) from a recognised institution
  • Unemployed: not in a government/private job or self-employment and not studying further

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who are in a job or self-employment
  • Those who are currently studying
  • Applicants below 20 or above 25 years

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Graduation certificate / marksheet
  • Residence certificate
  • Bank account details
  • ID proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Register on 7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in
  2. Fill personal and academic details and upload documents
  3. Get verified at the District Registration & Counselling Centre (DRCC) within 60 days
  4. After approval the allowance comes to the bank account
7nishchay-yuvaupmission.bihar.gov.in – portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much allowance is given?

₹1,000 per month for a maximum of 2 years.

Will a 12th pass get it?

The scheme was for 12th pass at first, but is now extended to graduate youth. See the latest eligibility on the portal.

Where is verification done?

At the district DRCC (District Registration & Counselling Centre), within 60 days of registration.
