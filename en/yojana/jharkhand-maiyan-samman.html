Mukhyamantri Maiyan Samman Yojana (Jharkhand)

👩 📍 Jharkhand · Dept of Women, Child Development & Social Security, Jharkhand · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly amount₹2,500
Age18 to 50 years (rules updated over time)
PaymentDBT, direct to account
Portalmmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Maiyan Samman is a Jharkhand Government scheme in which eligible women get ₹2,500 a month. Reports mention verification (eKYC) steps, so keep your account details correct.

Key benefits

  • ₹2,500 every month, that is ₹30,000 a year
  • Money goes directly to the Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Payment continues after verification

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A permanent woman resident of Jharkhand
  • Within the fixed age limit
  • A bank account linked to Aadhaar

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Women from income-tax-paying families
  • Women from government-employee families

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in or go to the nearest service centre/camp
  2. Fill the application and attach documents
  3. Complete verification and check the status on the portal
mmmsy.jharkhand.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much is paid?

₹2,500 every month.

What if the payment stops?

Check verification/eKYC and bank details and ask at the nearest office.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
