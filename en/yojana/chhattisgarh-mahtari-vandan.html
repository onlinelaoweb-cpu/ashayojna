Mahtari Vandan Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

👩 📍 Chhattisgarh · Women & Child Development Dept, Chhattisgarh · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly amount₹1,000
Per year₹12,000
Age21 years or above
Portalmahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Mahtari Vandan is a Chhattisgarh Government scheme that gives eligible married women of the state ₹1,000 every month directly in the bank account.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,000 every month, that is ₹12,000 a year
  • Direct DBT to the bank account
  • Categories like widows and deserted women are reported to be included

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A permanent woman resident of Chhattisgarh
  • Age 21 or above and married/widowed/deserted/divorced
  • A bank account linked to Aadhaar

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Women who are government employees or in their families
  • Women from income-tax-paying families

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Get the application form at the Anganwadi centre or the online portal
  2. Submit the form with the documents
  3. After verification your name comes in the beneficiary list

How to check your status

  1. Open mahtarivandan.cgstate.gov.in.
  2. As per third-party guides, the 'application status / payment status' option shows payment history when you enter the registered mobile or Aadhaar and the captcha (menu names may change).
  3. If e-KYC is pending, go to an Anganwadi centre or CSC with Aadhaar and the registered mobile.
  4. Check that the bank account number and IFSC on the portal are correct.

Payment not received? Possible reasons

  • If the bank account is not NPCI-mapped, the DBT fails. Ask the bank for 'Aadhaar seeding'.
  • Aadhaar is not linked to the bank account (NPCI mapping). Ask the bank branch, or check 'Bank Seeding Status' at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in (the menu name may change).
  • e-KYC / verification is pending. Complete it online, at a CSC or at the concerned office.
  • Name or date of birth is different in the application and in Aadhaar/bank records.
  • The bank account is closed or dormant, or the account number/IFSC on the portal is wrong.
  • The instalment was released late because of government approval, funds or an election code of conduct. In that case payments are held for everyone in the state, not only for you.
