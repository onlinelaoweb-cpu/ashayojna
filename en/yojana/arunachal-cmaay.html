Chief Minister Arogya Arunachal Yojana (CMAAY)

🏥 📍 Arunachal Pradesh · Dept of Health & Family Welfare, Arunachal Pradesh · Last checked: 15 November 2026

CoverUp to ₹5 lakh a year (family)
TreatmentCashless
Applies toEligible state residents
Central schemeAyushman Bharat also available
⚠️ This is based on reports and the link goes to the main state portal. Confirm eligibility and enrolment with the district health office.

What is the scheme?

CMAAY is the Arunachal Pradesh Government's health insurance scheme giving cashless treatment at listed hospitals. As per reports, the government has extended it to pensioners as well.

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh a year per family
  • Available at listed government and private hospitals
  • Works together with Ayushman Bharat

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A permanent resident of Arunachal Pradesh
  • Falling in the fixed eligibility category
  • Valid identity documents

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who are not residents of the state
  • Those outside the eligibility category

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Residence/Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC)
  • Family details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Ask at the nearest government hospital or district health office
  2. Get the card made after the eligibility check
  3. Show the card at a listed hospital for treatment
arunachalpradesh.gov.in – state portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much cover is there?

As per reports up to ₹5 lakh a year.

Where is the card made?

Find out from the district health office or a listed hospital.
