Subhadra Yojana (Odisha)

👩 📍 Odisha · Women & Child Development Dept, Odisha · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Yearly amount₹10,000
InstalmentsTwo of ₹5,000 each
Age21 to 60 years
Portalsubhadra.odisha.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Subhadra is an Odisha Government scheme in which eligible women get ₹10,000 a year in two equal instalments. As per reports the scheme provides a total of ₹50,000 over five years.

Key benefits

  • ₹10,000 a year, in two instalments
  • Money goes directly to the Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • New applications open from time to time

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A permanent woman resident of Odisha
  • Age 21 to 60 years
  • No government job or income-tax payer in the family

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Women from income-tax-paying families
  • Government employee/pensioner families

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card / food security card
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open subhadra.odisha.gov.in or go to the nearest Mo Seva/Jan Seva Kendra
  2. Fill the application and attach documents
  3. After verification the instalment is credited

How to check your status

  1. Open subhadra.odisha.gov.in or go to the nearest Mo Seva/Jan Seva Kendra.
  2. Choose the status or payment option and enter the application number/Aadhaar (whatever the portal asks).
  3. As per reports, instalments are released in phases. If your turn has not come yet, the payment may take time.

Payment not received? Possible reasons

  • As per reports, payments are released in phases, so some beneficiaries get them later.
  • Aadhaar is not linked to the bank account (NPCI mapping). Ask the bank branch, or check 'Bank Seeding Status' at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in (the menu name may change).
  • e-KYC / verification is pending. Complete it online, at a CSC or at the concerned office.
  • Name or date of birth is different in the application and in Aadhaar/bank records.
  • The bank account is closed or dormant, or the account number/IFSC on the portal is wrong.
  • The instalment was released late because of government approval, funds or an election code of conduct. In that case payments are held for everyone in the state, not only for you.
