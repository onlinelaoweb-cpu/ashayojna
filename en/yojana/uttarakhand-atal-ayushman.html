Atal Ayushman Uttarakhand Yojana

🏥 📍 Uttarakhand · State Health Authority, Uttarakhand · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Cover₹5 lakh a year (family)
TreatmentCashless
CoverageState resident families
Portalayushmanuttarakhand.org
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Atal Ayushman Uttarakhand is a state health scheme giving resident families cashless treatment at listed hospitals. It runs together with the Central Ayushman Bharat and, as per reports, the coverage is wide.

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh a year per family
  • Available at listed government and private hospitals
  • Works together with Ayushman Bharat

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A resident family of Uttarakhand
  • Aadhaar and ration card/identity documents
  • Completing the fixed enrolment process

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who are not residents of the state
  • Those whose enrolment is incomplete

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card / residence proof
  • Family details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open ayushmanuttarakhand.org or go to the nearest CSC/hospital
  2. Enrol with Aadhaar and ID documents
  3. After the card is made take treatment at a listed hospital

Frequently asked questions

How much cover is there?

Up to ₹5 lakh a year cashless.

Where is the card made?

At a CSC or a listed hospital.
