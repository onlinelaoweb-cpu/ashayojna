Bihar Student Credit Card Yojana

🎓 📍 Bihar · Education Department, Bihar (Saat Nishchay-2) · Last checked: 12 November 2026

LoanUp to ₹4 lakh
GuaranteeNo guarantee
EligibleResident of Bihar, 12th pass
Portal7nishchay-yojana.bihar.gov.in
⚠️ Confirm interest rate, repayment period and conditions with the portal/bank. The information here is general.

What is the scheme?

Under the Bihar Student Credit Card scheme, student residents of Bihar can take an education loan of up to ₹4 lakh, without guarantee, for higher, technical or professional courses.

Key benefits

  • Education loan up to ₹4 lakh
  • Without any guarantee/security
  • Repayment after the course is completed (as per rules)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Permanent resident of Bihar
  • 12th pass and admitted to a recognised institution
  • Course/institution covered by the scheme
  • Age limit and other conditions on the portal

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those studying outside the scheme's course/institution list
  • Those who are not residents of Bihar

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Residence certificate
  • 12th marksheet
  • Proof of admission / fee details
  • Bank account details
  • Passport photo

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to 7nishchay-yojana.bihar.gov.in
  2. Register for the Student Credit Card
  3. Fill the application and upload documents
  4. After checks at district level the application is sent to the bank
  5. After the bank's sanction, the fee goes to the institution/account
7nishchay-yojana.bihar.gov.in – portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much loan is given?

Education loan up to ₹4 lakh.

Is a guarantee needed?

No, the scheme is without guarantee.
