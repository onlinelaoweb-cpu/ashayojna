Bihar 125 Units Free Electricity Scheme

💡 📍 Bihar · Energy Department, Bihar · Last checked: 11 November 2026

Free electricity125 units per month
For whomAll household consumers of the state
StartFrom 1 August 2025 (from the July bill)
SolarUnder Kutir Jyoti, government to bear full cost for the poorest families
⚠️ This is based on the announcement, and confirmed information on consumption above 125 units, the application process etc. is not on this page. Confirm with the electricity company.

What is the scheme?

From 1 August 2025 the Bihar Government announced up to 125 units a month of free electricity for all household consumers in the state. There is also a plan to set up solar plants on rooftops or nearby government land over the next three years, and the government will bear the full cost of solar for the poorest Kutir Jyoti families.

Key benefits

  • Up to 125 units of electricity free every month
  • Target of reaching about 1.67 crore families
  • Help for solar plants (government to bear the full cost for the poorest Kutir Jyoti families)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Household electricity consumers of Bihar
  • A valid household electricity connection
  • Confirm with your electricity company whether a separate application is needed

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Commercial or industrial connections
  • Families without a valid connection

Documents required

  • Electricity bill / consumer number
  • Aadhaar card (if asked)
  • For solar: proof of house/roof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check your electricity bill: the amount should be zero for consumption up to 125 units
  2. For information or complaints contact your electricity company (NBPDCL North Bihar / SBPDCL South Bihar)
  3. For solar, apply to your electricity company or as per the scheme's announcement
sbpdcl.co.in / nbpdcl.co.in – electricity companies ↗

Frequently asked questions

Will every home get 125 units free?

It has been announced for all household consumers of the state. Check on your bill.

What if I use more than 125 units?

Reports say no fixed charges apply up to 125 units. For how the bill is worked out above that, get confirmed information from your electricity company.

Do I have to apply?

As per SBPDCL the benefit is adjusted automatically in the smart-meter system (prepaid and postpaid). No separate application was mentioned. Confirm with the electricity company.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
