Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (Bihar)

📚 📍 Bihar · Social Welfare Department, Bihar · Last checked: 12 November 2026

On passing Intermediate₹25,000 (released in July 2026 for 2026 passers)
On passing graduation₹50,000 (as reported, not freshly confirmed)
ConditionUnmarried girl student, resident of Bihar
Portalmedhasoft.bih.nic.in
⚠️ Amounts and eligibility have changed over time, and the information here is based on news sources. Confirm on the portal before applying.

What is the scheme?

Kanya Utthan is a Bihar Government scheme that gives unmarried girl students a cash incentive on completing Intermediate and graduation, so daughters can study further.

Key benefits

  • ₹25,000 on passing Intermediate (the Education Department released the amount in July 2026 for unmarried girls who passed in 2026)
  • ₹50,000 on passing graduation (as reported)
  • Amount goes directly to the girl's bank account

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Girl student who is a permanent resident of Bihar
  • Must be unmarried
  • Passed Intermediate/graduation from a recognised institution

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Married students
  • Those who did not complete studies or meet the conditions

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank passbook (in her own name)
  • Marksheet and certificates
  • Residence certificate
  • Self-declaration of being unmarried
  • College proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to medhasoft.bih.nic.in
  2. Choose the scheme (Inter/graduation) and register
  3. Fill the application and upload documents
  4. After verification by college/department the amount comes to the account
medhasoft.bih.nic.in – portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much money does Kanya Utthan give?

As reported, ₹25,000 on passing Intermediate and ₹50,000 on passing graduation. Confirm the amount on the portal.

Will a married student get it?

No, she must be unmarried.

When to apply?

After passing the exam, in the portal's open window. See dates on the portal.
