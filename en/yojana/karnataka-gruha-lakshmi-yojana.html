Gruha Lakshmi Yojana (Karnataka)

👩 📍 Karnataka · Women & Child Development Department, Karnataka · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly amount₹2,000
BeneficiariesAbout 1.26 crore (report)
StatusUnder review, some payments reported pending
ApplySeva Sindhu
⚠️ This scheme is amid news of a review and pending payments. The full conditions and application status could not be checked here; confirm with Seva Sindhu or the local office.

What is the scheme?

Gruha Lakshmi is one of Karnataka's guarantee schemes, giving the woman head of the family ₹2,000 a month. In 2026 the government began reviewing schemes, and a November 2026 report says payments for some months are pending.

Key benefits

  • ₹2,000 a month directly to the bank account (DBT)
  • About 1.26 crore beneficiaries (per one report)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Woman of Karnataka who is the head of the family (see the Seva Sindhu portal for exact conditions)
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • The government has said benefits will go to those on the voters' list

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those found to pay income tax or GST (the government is verifying)
  • Those who have died or do not meet conditions (reports mention payments to such accounts)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Residence proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check the scheme status on sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in
  2. Ask the local office/portal whether new applications or re-verification are needed
  3. Check payment status in your bank account or on the portal
sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in – Seva Sindhu ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much does Gruha Lakshmi give?

₹2,000 a month.

Are payments pending?

A November 2026 report says the government told the High Court that financial constraints led to some months' payments being pending. Confirm the latest status.

Is the scheme being stopped?

A July 2026 report says schemes are under review. We did not find any announcement of closure.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
