Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

🤱 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Women & Child Development · Last checked: 11 November 2026

First child₹5,000 (₹3,000 + ₹2,000)
Second girl child₹6,000 (one instalment, after birth)
ApplyAnganwadi / health centre / pmmvy.wcd.gov.in
Income limitNone
⚠️ The number of eligible children and the amounts may change by government order. Confirm with the Anganwadi or pmmvy.wcd.gov.in before applying.

What is the scheme?

PMMVY gives cash support to pregnant and breastfeeding mothers for rest and nutrition. For the first living child a fixed amount is paid in two instalments, and if the second child is a girl, ₹6,000 is paid in one go. The money comes to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Key benefits

  • Total ₹5,000 for the first child: ₹3,000 (on pregnancy registration) and ₹2,000 (after at least one antenatal check-up)
  • ₹6,000 in one instalment if the second child is a girl
  • A separate incentive for institutional delivery may be available under Janani Suraksha Yojana

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Pregnant/breastfeeding woman for the first living child
  • Pregnancy must be registered at an Anganwadi centre or recognised health facility
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • No income limit

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Women employed in Central/State government or PSUs
  • Those already getting similar maternity benefit under another law
  • For the third or later child

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank passbook
  • Mother-Child Protection (MCP) card
  • Proof of pregnancy registration
  • ID proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
  • Child's birth certificate (for later instalment)
  • Consent/declaration form

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Register the pregnancy at the nearest Anganwadi centre or health centre (PHC/CHC) and get an MCP card
  2. Fill the PMMVY application form and submit documents
  3. The Anganwadi worker/ASHA-ANM enters details on the portal
  4. Online: you can also apply on pmmvy.wcd.gov.in or the UMANG app; keep the reference number safe
  5. After checks, instalments come to the Aadhaar-linked account
pmmvy.wcd.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much does PMMVY pay?

₹5,000 (₹3,000 + ₹2,000) for the first child and ₹6,000 if the second child is a girl.

Do government employees get it?

No, women working in Central/State government or PSUs are not eligible.

Whom to contact for help?

Your Anganwadi worker or the block PMMVY nodal officer, or lodge a complaint on pmmvy.wcd.gov.in.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
