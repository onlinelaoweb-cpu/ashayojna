Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)

📤 Share:

Photo: Subhashis Das / Unsplash

🏘️ 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs · Last checked: 21 November 2026

PeriodFive years from 1 Sept 2024 (an official end date was not confirmed)
BLC / AHP assistanceUp to ₹2.50 lakh (PIB, 9 Aug 2026)
Interest subsidy (ISS)Maximum ₹1.80 lakh; rate reported as 4% (on first ₹8 lakh)
Income limitEWS ₹3 lakh · LIG ₹3–6 lakh · MIG ₹6–9 lakh (yearly)
Sanctioned so far18.38 lakh houses under 2.0 (PIB, 9 Aug 2026)

📚 Sources and what we checked

Sources checked (21 Nov 2026): pmaymis.gov.in operational guidelines and FAQ, PIB (9 Aug 2026), MoHUA release (17 Jun 2026), Lok Sabha replies (Dec 2025, Feb 2026). pmay-urban.gov.in could not be opened directly. No rule change for 2025-26 was found.

✅ Eligibility📄 Documents📝 How to apply❓ FAQ

What is the scheme?

PMAY-U 2.0 aims to provide homes to 1 crore urban families. It runs in four verticals: Beneficiary-Led Construction on own land (BLC), Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP), Affordable Rental Housing (ARH) and Interest Subsidy Scheme on home loans (ISS). One vertical is chosen at application and cannot be changed later. As per PIB (9 Aug 2026) there are four verticals and 18.38 lakh houses had been sanctioned up to the meeting of 6 Aug 2026 (14.40 lakh BLC, 2.48 lakh AHP, 1.36 lakh ISS, 13,046 ARH). A June 2026 ministry release says about 97% of houses are in women's names.

Key benefits

  • BLC (building on own vacant land, EWS): up to ₹2.50 lakh
  • AHP (buying a house in a project, EWS): up to ₹2.50 lakh per unit
  • ARH: rental homes for urban migrants and low-income workers
  • ISS (EWS/LIG/MIG): subsidy applies to loans up to ₹25 lakh and houses up to ₹35 lakh; maximum subsidy ₹1.80 lakh. The subsidy rate (4% on the first ₹8 lakh) and tenure (up to 12 years) came only from one January 2025 report, so confirm with your bank
  • For BLC/AHP the house carpet area is 30 to 45 sq m (per the operational guidelines)
  • ARH provides ₹3,000 per sq m from the Centre plus ₹2,000 from the state (per the guidelines; this is for project developers)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Annual family income: EWS up to ₹3 lakh, LIG ₹3–6 lakh, MIG ₹6–9 lakh
  • No family member owns a pucca house anywhere in India
  • Families who took an earlier housing scheme benefit are generally not eligible (about a 20-year condition; see official rules)
  • An Indian citizen living in an urban area; family means husband, wife and unmarried children
  • A family can take the benefit of either PMAY-G or PMAY-U 2.0, not both (per the guidelines)

Tick the conditions that apply to you. This is only a rough guide; the department makes the final decision.

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Families that own a pucca house anywhere
  • Families that took an earlier housing scheme benefit (as per rules)
  • Families who got a house from any government housing scheme in the last 20 years
  • ISS is given only once per property
  • NRIs are not eligible (per the guidelines)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card (of family members)
  • Income proof / income declaration
  • Bank account details
  • House/land papers (land ownership for BLC)
  • Documents asked by the bank for a home loan
  • Signed eligibility undertaking
  • Caste/community proof (where applicable)
  • Affidavit of not owning a pucca house (for BLC)
  • PAN and address proof (for ISS)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check your income and eligibility, confirm you have no pucca house
  2. Pick the right vertical for you (ISS if you have a home loan)
  3. Apply on the official portal of the Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs or through an authorised bank/lender
  4. Ask the lender how the subsidy will be credited to your loan account
  5. Track the application with your assessment ID on PMAY-MIS / PMAY-U 2.0 citizen portal
  6. You can apply on the portal, at a Common Service Centre (CSC) or at your urban local body (municipality) (per the pmaymis FAQ)
  7. In BLC the urban local body prepares the project, then state and central committees sanction it; in ISS, after you register on the portal the lender must claim the subsidy within 30 days of disbursal (per the FAQ)

Frequently asked questions

Is there a subsidy on home loans?

Yes, under the ISS vertical EWS/LIG/MIG families get 4% interest subsidy on the first ₹8 lakh of the loan, maximum ₹1.80 lakh.

Can I apply in more than one vertical?

No, you must choose one vertical at the time of application and it cannot be changed later.

Over how many years is the subsidy given?

Different sources give different timelines (5 to 12 years). Ask your bank/lender for the official rule.

Is an end date announced?

The operational guidelines say five years from 1 Sept 2024, i.e. about 2029; a December 2025 Lok Sabha reply also says 'next five years'. One report mentioned an extension to 30 Sept 2026 but we found no official confirmation; check the portal before applying.

Can I get both PMAY-G and PMAY-U 2.0?

No, as per the guidelines a family can take the benefit of only one of them.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
