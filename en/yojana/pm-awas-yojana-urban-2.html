Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)

🏘️ 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Period2024 to 2029 (started 1 Sept 2024)
BLC / AHP assistanceUp to ₹2.50 lakh
Interest subsidy (ISS)4% on first ₹8 lakh of loan
Income limitEWS ₹3 lakh · LIG ₹3–6 lakh · MIG ₹6–9 lakh (yearly)
⚠️ Subsidy conditions and period depend on government orders. Confirm with the official portal and your bank before applying.

What is the scheme?

PMAY-U 2.0 aims to provide homes to 1 crore urban families. It runs in four verticals: Beneficiary-Led Construction on own land (BLC), Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP), Affordable Rental Housing (ARH) and Interest Subsidy Scheme on home loans (ISS). One vertical is chosen at application and cannot be changed later.

Key benefits

  • BLC (building on own vacant land, EWS): up to ₹2.50 lakh
  • AHP (buying a house in a project, EWS): up to ₹2.50 lakh per unit
  • ARH: rental homes for urban migrants and low-income workers
  • ISS (EWS/LIG/MIG): 4% interest subsidy on first ₹8 lakh of home loan; limit of ₹25 lakh loan and ₹35 lakh house value for subsidy; maximum subsidy ₹1.80 lakh

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Annual family income: EWS up to ₹3 lakh, LIG ₹3–6 lakh, MIG ₹6–9 lakh
  • No family member owns a pucca house anywhere in India
  • Families who took an earlier housing scheme benefit are generally not eligible (about a 20-year condition; see official rules)

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Families that own a pucca house anywhere
  • Families that took an earlier housing scheme benefit (as per rules)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card (of family members)
  • Income proof / income declaration
  • Bank account details
  • House/land papers (land ownership for BLC)
  • Documents asked by the bank for a home loan

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check your income and eligibility, confirm you have no pucca house
  2. Pick the right vertical for you (ISS if you have a home loan)
  3. Apply on the official portal of the Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs or through an authorised bank/lender
  4. Ask the lender how the subsidy will be credited to your loan account
  5. Track the application with your assessment ID on PMAY-MIS / PMAY-U 2.0 citizen portal
pmay-urban.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

Is there a subsidy on home loans?

Yes, under the ISS vertical EWS/LIG/MIG families get 4% interest subsidy on the first ₹8 lakh of the loan, maximum ₹1.80 lakh.

Can I apply in more than one vertical?

No, you must choose one vertical at the time of application and it cannot be changed later.

Over how many years is the subsidy given?

Different sources give different timelines (5 to 12 years). Ask your bank/lender for the official rule.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
