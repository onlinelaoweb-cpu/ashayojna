Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PM Internship)

🧑‍💼 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Stipend (pilot)₹5,000 a month
One-time help₹6,000
Family incomeBelow ₹8 lakh
Portalpminternship.mca.gov.in
⚠️ The scheme is a pilot and its 2026 rules (age, stipend, duration) have been changing. Confirm on the portal before applying.

What is the scheme?

Under the PM Internship Scheme young people get internship experience in top companies with a monthly stipend from the government. The scheme is in a pilot phase and the 2026 rules are reported to be changing.

Key benefits

  • Pilot rule: ₹5,000 a month stipend (₹4,500 from the government and ₹500 from the company), for 12 months
  • ₹6,000 one-time help on joining
  • A 2026 update of ₹9,000 a month and 6 to 9 months is reported, but formal confirmation is said to be pending

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Age about 21 to 24 per the pilot rules (one place says 18 to 25, check the portal)
  • Class 10, 12, ITI, diploma or graduate (BA, BSc, BCom, BCA, BBA, BPharma)
  • Not in full-time employment or full-time education
  • Family annual income below ₹8 lakh
  • No family member is a permanent government employee

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Graduates of IIT, IIM, NLU, IISER, NID, IIIT
  • Holders of CA, CMA, CS, MBBS, BDS, MBA or master's/higher degrees
  • Those already in a government skill/apprenticeship/internship scheme

Documents required

  • Aadhaar (e-KYC)
  • Educational certificates
  • Bank account details
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. On pminternship.mca.gov.in click 'Youth Registration' and enter your mobile number
  2. Verify the OTP and create a password
  3. Log in and complete your profile (e-KYC, education, bank, skills)
  4. Under 'Internship Opportunity' filter and apply for up to 3 internships
  5. Accept the declarations, submit and track under 'My Applications'
pminternship.mca.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much is the stipend?

₹5,000 a month in the pilot. ₹9,000 for 2026 is reported; confirm on the portal.

Are applications open now?

One source listed the last date as April 2026 (expected), which may have passed. Check the latest round on the portal.

How long is the internship?

12 months in the pilot; the 2026 announcement is 6 to 9 months.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
