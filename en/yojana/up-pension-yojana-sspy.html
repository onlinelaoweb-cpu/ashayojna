Uttar Pradesh Pension Scheme – Old Age, Widow & Disability (SSPY)

👵 📍 Uttar Pradesh · Social Welfare Department, Uttar Pradesh · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Monthly pension₹1,000 per month
PaymentQuarterly (₹3,000), by DBT
CategoriesOld age, destitute woman/widow, disabled
Portalsspy-up.gov.in
⚠️ Income limit, age conditions and verification rules may change. Do the final check of the information here on sspy-up.gov.in.

What is the scheme?

Under the Social Security Pension Scheme (SSPY) the Uttar Pradesh Government pays ₹1,000 a month to eligible elderly people, destitute women and persons with disabilities. The money comes directly to an Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,000 pension per month
  • Paid quarterly as ₹3,000 into the bank account (DBT)
  • Three categories: old age (60+), destitute woman/widow, disabled (40% or more disability)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
  • Old-age pension: age 60 or above
  • Widow/destitute woman pension: proof of husband's death and the scheme's age conditions
  • Disability pension: certificate of 40% or more disability
  • Family income within the scheme limit (see exact limit on the portal) and not receiving any other government pension

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those already receiving another government pension
  • Those whose income or status is outside the scheme conditions

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank passbook
  • Income certificate
  • Age proof / birth certificate
  • Residence certificate
  • Husband's death certificate for widows; 40%+ disability certificate for the disabled

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open sspy-up.gov.in
  2. Choose 'Apply Online' and select the type of pension
  3. Fill Aadhaar, bank and personal details
  4. Upload documents and submit
  5. After verification at gram panchayat/ward level the pension is sanctioned

Frequently asked questions

How much does UP pension give?

₹1,000 per month, paid as ₹3,000 quarterly into the bank account.

Is there a fee to apply?

No. Applying on the official portal is free.

What if the pension stops?

Check Aadhaar-bank linking/DBT, verification status and account details. If needed contact the District Social Welfare Office.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
