Bihar Social Security Pension – ₹1,100 a month (elderly, widow, disabled)

👴 📍 Bihar · Social Welfare Department, Bihar · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Monthly pension₹1,100 (earlier ₹400)
CategoriesElderly, widow (Laxmibai), disabled
Conditione-KYC and Aadhaar-seeded bank account
Portalsspmis.bihar.gov.in
⚠️ Eligibility and e-KYC dates can change. See the latest rules on sspmis.bihar.gov.in.

What is the scheme?

The Bihar Government has raised the social security pension from ₹400 to ₹1,100 a month. It applies to old-age, widow and disability pensions. Payment is by DBT and the next instalment can be held if e-KYC is not done.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,100 pension per month
  • Directly to the bank account (DBT)
  • Applies to all three categories: elderly, widow and disabled

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Resident of Bihar
  • Old-age pension: generally age 60+
  • Widow and disability pensions: the scheme's eligibility conditions
  • Bank account linked to Aadhaar and seeded for DBT

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those whose e-KYC is incomplete (payment may stop)
  • Those receiving another pension or not meeting conditions

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank passbook
  • Age proof
  • Husband's death certificate for widows; disability certificate for the disabled
  • Residence certificate

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to sspmis.bihar.gov.in and choose the type of pension
  2. Fill the application and upload documents
  3. After the pension is sanctioned, get e-KYC done: e-Labharthi (elabharthi.bih.nic.in), CSC or the block social security cell
  4. Check payment status on the portal
sspmis.bihar.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much pension does Bihar give?

₹1,100 per month.

What if the pension stops?

The common reason is e-KYC or Aadhaar-bank seeding. Complete e-KYC and get NPCI/DBT seeding confirmed by the bank.

Where to do e-KYC?

On the e-Labharthi/SSPMIS portal, at the nearest CSC or the block social security cell, in person.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
