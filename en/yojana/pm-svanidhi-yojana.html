PM SVANidhi Yojana (for street vendors)

🛒 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs · Last checked: 12 November 2026

LoanWorking-capital loan in three tranches
Next trancheOn repaying the previous one
Scheme periodExtended to March 2030
Credit cardUPI-linked RuPay card after repaying the second tranche
⚠️ Tranche amounts and conditions changed after restructuring, so amounts are not given here. Get the latest from pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in or your bank.

What is the scheme?

PM SVANidhi is for street, cart and hawker vendors. It gives collateral-free working-capital loans in three tranches, and repaying each on time opens the way to the next, larger one. The scheme has been extended to March 2030 and widened to census towns and surrounding areas.

Key benefits

  • Collateral-free working-capital loan in three tranches
  • Eligibility for the next tranche on repaying each one
  • After repaying the second tranche, a chance to apply for a UPI-linked RuPay credit card
  • Incentives such as cashback on digital transactions (amounts change from time to time)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Vendors doing street/hawking work in urban areas
  • Identification by the state/urban local body (vending certificate / ID card or name in the survey) is required
  • The scheme now covers census towns and areas around urban centres

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those not recorded in an ID proof or survey (they must first register with the local body)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Vending certificate / ID card (from local body)
  • Bank account details
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Find out your status of identification (survey/certificate) as a vendor with your municipal corporation/council
  2. Apply on pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in or through a bank/CSC
  3. The bank/lender sanctions the loan after verification
  4. Repay on time to unlock the next tranche and the credit card option
pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much loan does SVANidhi give?

A working-capital loan in three tranches, with the next given on repaying each. See the exact tranche amounts with the bank or on the portal, as they changed after restructuring.

Can I get it without a vending certificate?

Generally identification by the local body or a name in the survey is needed. Contact your municipal corporation/council.

How do I get the credit card?

Vendors who fully repay the second tranche can apply for a UPI-linked RuPay credit card.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
