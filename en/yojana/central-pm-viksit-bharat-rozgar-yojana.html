PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY) – Up to ₹15,000 on Your First Job

What is the scheme?

The PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY) was earlier known as the Employment Linked Incentive (ELI) scheme. The Union Cabinet approved it on 1 July 2025 and the outlay is given as ₹99,446 crore. As per PIB (Labour Ministry, 18 Dec 2025) a first-time employee gets an amount equal to one month's EPF wage, up to ₹15,000, in two instalments, and the employer gets up to ₹3,000 a month for each additional employee. As per a PMO release, incentives of about ₹2,400 crore were disbursed on 19 June 2026. The aim is to create more than 3.5 crore jobs over two years. This page is for information; nobody can 'register' you for this against payment.

Key benefits

  • As per PIB, a first-time employee (in both existing and new establishments) gets an incentive equal to one month's EPF wage, maximum ₹15,000
  • First instalment (up to ₹7,500) after 6 months of continuous employment
  • Second instalment after 12 months of employment and completing the prescribed financial literacy programme; it is credited to a savings account/instrument for a specified period
  • Employers get up to ₹3,000 a month per additional employee kept for at least 6 months; benefits run four years in manufacturing and two years in other sectors
  • Kotak Neo (19 Oct 2026) says payment is made directly (DBT/ABPS) to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A first-time employee entering an EPF-covered formal job (new UAN)
  • Monthly wage up to ₹1 lakh (PIB New India Samachar Jul 2026 and Kotak Neo)
  • The job must have started between 1 Aug 2025 and 31 Jul 2027
  • An Aadhaar-seeded bank account and completion of the financial literacy programme for the second instalment

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • People who already have a UAN/EPF account and are not first-time entrants (reports count 're-entrants' separately; we could not confirm any benefit for them)
  • Those drawing more than ₹1 lakh a month
  • Someone who leaves before 6 months will not get the first instalment (6-month continuous service condition)
  • Employees of establishments that are not registered with EPFO

Documents required

  • Aadhaar and Aadhaar-linked mobile number
  • UAN (to be generated/activated when you join)
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank account (account number and IFSC)
  • For employers (as per ClearTax): EPFO establishment ID, PAN, GSTIN/TAN, PAN-linked bank account, monthly ECRs

How to apply (step by step)

  1. When you join, ask the employer to create your UAN and start EPF deposits; a new UAN is generated for a first job
  2. As per ClearTax the UAN is generated/activated through Aadhaar Face Authentication in the UMANG app
  3. Link your Aadhaar and bank account (ABPS) so the payment can be received
  4. As per ClearTax the employee does not need a separate application; eligibility is checked from EPFO records. Some sites such as Bajaj Finserv describe applying in the UMANG app; sources disagree, so ask EPFO or your employer first
  5. After 12 months complete the financial literacy programme; only then is the second instalment paid
  6. Check the status in your EPFO passbook/portal. Do not pay any agent

Frequently asked questions

Is the scheme running now?

Yes. As per a PMO release incentives of about ₹2,400 crore were disbursed on 19 June 2026, and the scheme period runs to 31 July 2027.

How much money and when?

One month's EPF wage, maximum ₹15,000. The first instalment (up to ₹7,500) after 6 months, the second after 12 months and completion of financial literacy.

Do government employees get it?

The scheme is for EPFO-covered formal jobs. We found no clear rule on government jobs in any source.

Is there anything for employers?

Yes, up to ₹3,000 a month per additional employee. As per ClearTax it is ₹1,000, ₹2,000 or ₹3,000 by wage slab, with a minimum of 2 (under 50 staff) or 5 (50 or more) additional hires; this is a single source, so verify before relying on it.

What if the money does not come?

First check that your UAN is new, Aadhaar-bank seeding is done and the employer has filed EPF/ECR. Then ask EPFO or your employer. We found no separate scheme helpline.
