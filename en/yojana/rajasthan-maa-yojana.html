Mukhyamantri Ayushman Arogya (MAA) Yojana (Rajasthan)

🏥 📍 Rajasthan · Medical & Health Department, Rajasthan · Last checked: 12 November 2026

CoverCashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh
CardMAA scheme card required
HospitalsListed hospitals in Rajasthan, and hospitals in other states via PM-JAY portability
Packages2,179 treatment packages (March 2026 report)
⚠️ Eligibility and the exact card process could not be verified here. The information is based on March 2026 news, so confirm with the health department.

What is the scheme?

MAA is a Rajasthan Government health scheme under which residents can get cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh by showing the MAA card. It is described as cover above the Centre's Ayushman Bharat (₹5 lakh).

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh
  • At listed government and private hospitals in Rajasthan
  • Treatment in hospitals of other states via PM-JAY portability (about 31,000 hospitals as per a March 2026 report)
  • Packages from minor surgeries to serious illnesses (e.g. PTCA, dialysis, kidney stones, caesarean)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Resident of Rajasthan
  • MAA scheme card is required
  • Exact income or category conditions are not confirmed on this page; confirm with the Medical & Health Department

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those without a MAA card
  • Treatments or hospitals outside the list

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Jan Aadhaar card
  • MAA card
  • ID proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Ask at a nearby government hospital, e-Mitra or the health department how to get a MAA card
  2. For treatment show the card and an ID at a listed hospital
  3. The hospital starts treatment after approval from the Rajasthan State Health Assurance Agency

Cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh is stated.

Can I get treatment in another state?

As per reports, treatment is possible at listed hospitals in several states via PM-JAY portability.
