Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyan – CM YUVA (Uttar Pradesh)

🚀 📍 Uttar Pradesh · MSME Department, Uttar Pradesh · Last checked: 11 November 2026

Age21 to 40 years
LoanUp to ₹5 lakh, interest-free
Subsidy10% margin money subsidy on project cost
Portalmsme.up.gov.in
⚠️ Different sources give different loan amounts and age limits (some banks quote lower stage-wise limits). The figures here are taken from the UP MSME portal, but still confirm on msme.up.gov.in before applying.

What is the scheme?

CM YUVA (Mukhyamantri Yuva Udyami Vikas Abhiyan) gives young people in Uttar Pradesh interest-free and collateral-free loans to start their own business. In every district, experts help from application to running the business. Applications are made on the UP MSME portal.

Key benefits

  • Interest-free loan up to ₹5 lakh (as per UP MSME portal)
  • Without collateral
  • 10% margin money subsidy on project cost, which eases the initial burden
  • After repaying the first loan a larger loan may be available in the next stage (confirm the amount with portal/bank)
  • District-level experts/guidance and ready project reports

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Resident of Uttar Pradesh
  • Age 21 to 40 years
  • At least 8th pass, or training/certificate under a government scheme or skill mission, a diploma or a degree
  • Has not taken interest or capital subsidy under any Central/State scheme before (except PM SVANidhi, as per bank)
  • Wanting to start a new enterprise

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Applicants below 21 or above 40 years (some reports mention the age limit changing, confirm on the portal)
  • Those who do not meet the eligibility conditions
  • Those who have not repaid an earlier loan

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Residence proof
  • Educational qualification / training or skill certificate
  • Bank account details
  • Project report / business plan
  • Other documents asked by the bank

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to msme.up.gov.in and look for the CM YUVA application
  2. Register and choose or upload your business idea/project report
  3. Fill the online application and submit documents
  4. After bank/department checks the loan is sanctioned
  5. Take guidance from the district expert
msme.up.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

What age is required for CM YUVA?

21 to 40 years as per the UP MSME portal. Some reports mention the limit changing, so confirm on the portal before applying.

Is it really interest-free?

The portal describes an interest-free, collateral-free loan up to ₹5 lakh. The bank's own conditions and checks still apply.

What is margin money subsidy?

A 10% subsidy on project cost that reduces the initial capital burden. Take details from the portal/bank.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
