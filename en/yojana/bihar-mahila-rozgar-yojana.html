Mukhyamantri Mahila Rozgar Yojana (Bihar)

👩‍🌾 📍 Bihar · Rural Development Department, Bihar (JEEViKA) · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Starting support₹10,000 (DBT)
Further supportUp to ₹2 lakh (after six months, based on progress)
BeneficiariesMainly women of JEEViKA self-help groups
Run byBihar Rural Livelihoods Promotion Society (JEEViKA)
⚠️ Confirm exact eligibility and the application process with JEEViKA or the district office. The information here is based on news sources.

What is the scheme?

Mukhyamantri Mahila Rozgar Yojana is a Bihar Government scheme that gives women ₹10,000 first to start a small livelihood or business and, once progress is seen, up to ₹2 lakh more. It is run through JEEViKA.

Key benefits

  • Starting ₹10,000, directly to the bank account (DBT)
  • Up to ₹2 lakh additional help on review of work after six months
  • Helps JEEViKA didis take up livelihoods and grow their work

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Women of Bihar, mainly linked to JEEViKA self-help groups (SHG)
  • Confirm exact eligibility conditions (age, one member per family etc.) with JEEViKA/district office

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who do not meet the scheme conditions
  • Further support is not given where the review of work progress falls short

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Bank account details (for DBT)
  • Proof of JEEViKA SHG membership
  • Passport photo and mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Ask your JEEViKA group/block JEEViKA office about the scheme's status and application dates
  2. Apply through the portal/group and submit documents
  3. After verification ₹10,000 comes to the bank account
  4. After a review of work progress, a decision on further support is made

Frequently asked questions

How much is the first instalment?

₹10,000, directly to the bank account.

When is the ₹2 lakh given?

After six months, based on a review of work progress, up to a maximum of ₹2 lakh.

Where to apply?

At your JEEViKA group or the block JEEViKA office.
