What is the scheme?

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi is a Central Government scheme that gives eligible farmer families ₹6,000 a year as direct income support for farming needs. The money goes without any middleman into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account. As per GKToday (31 Oct 2026) and Drishti IAS (3 Nov 2026, citing PIB) the 24th instalment was released on 30 October 2026 from Banaskantha, Gujarat, reaching about 9.34 crore farmer families at ₹2,000 each. GKToday gives the total as more than ₹18,680 crore and Drishti as about ₹18,690 crore, so treat it as roughly ₹18,700 crore. As per a Deccan Herald report the Cabinet has approved continuing the scheme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 (total ₹3,15,614 crore; the exact approval date was not visible on that page). The 23rd instalment came on 20 June 2026. We found no date for the 25th instalment.

Key benefits

  • ₹6,000 per year in total
  • Three instalments of ₹2,000, directly to the bank account (DBT)
  • Support for small farming costs such as seeds and fertiliser
  • The 24th instalment was released on 30 October 2026 (about 9.34 crore farmer families, roughly ₹18,700 crore in total; the two sources differ by ₹10 crore)
  • As per Deccan Herald the Cabinet has approved continuing the scheme from 2026-27 to 2030-31

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Indian citizen from a farmer family owning cultivable land
  • Land must be recorded in the farmer's own name in the state land records
  • 'Family' means husband, wife and minor children
  • For joint holdings, only one family member gets the benefit

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Institutional landholders (trusts, companies etc.)
  • Families where any member paid income tax in the last assessment year
  • Current or former ministers, MPs, MLAs, mayors
  • Serving or retired government employees (except Group D / MTS) and those with pension of ₹10,000 or more per month
  • Registered doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects
  • Tenant farmers who do not own land
  • As per a notice on the portal, the department has temporarily withheld instalments in some 'suspected cases' until physical verification is done. These include farmers who became land owners after 1 Feb 2019 and cases where more than one family member (e.g. both husband and wife, or an adult and a minor) is getting the benefit

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land papers (khatauni / khasra / passbook)
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account (with IFSC)
  • Active mobile number (for OTP)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open pmkisan.gov.in and go to 'Farmers Corner'
  2. Choose 'New Farmer Registration' and enter Aadhaar and mobile number
  3. Fill state, land and bank details correctly and submit
  4. Complete e-KYC: enter Aadhaar in 'eKYC' and verify by OTP (if it fails, do biometric e-KYC at the nearest CSC)
  5. Keep checking 'Know Your Status'; village lists are under 'Beneficiary List'
  6. Check status under 'Know Your Status' with your registration number; the OTP goes to the mobile registered for e-KYC. The portal also mentions the 'Kisan eMitra' chatbot
  7. Biometric e-KYC can be done at a CSC, in the PM-KISAN mobile app or through a state-authorised login (as per the portal)

How to check your status

  1. Open pmkisan.gov.in and choose 'Know Your Status' under 'Farmers Corner'.
  2. Enter the registration number and captcha. If you have forgotten the number, use 'Know your registration no.' there with your mobile number.
  3. See each instalment and the status of 'e-KYC', 'Land Seeding' and 'Aadhaar Bank Account Seeding' (menu names may change).
  4. If it is unclear, ask on helpline 155261 or 011-24300606.

Payment not received? Possible reasons

  • As per reports, the instalment can be held if e-KYC (OTP/biometric/face) is pending.
  • Land records are not seeded or verified on the portal. Ask the patwari/agriculture office.
  • Aadhaar-based DBT (NPCI) is not active on the bank account.
  • Aadhaar is not linked to the bank account (NPCI mapping). Ask the bank branch, or check 'Bank Seeding Status' at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in (the menu name may change).
  • e-KYC / verification is pending. Complete it online, at a CSC or at the concerned office.
  • Name or date of birth is different in the application and in Aadhaar/bank records.
  • The bank account is closed or dormant, or the account number/IFSC on the portal is wrong.
  • The instalment was released late because of government approval, funds or an election code of conduct. In that case payments are held for everyone in the state, not only for you.

Frequently asked questions

How much money does PM Kisan give?

₹6,000 a year to an eligible family, as three instalments of ₹2,000.

Why is my instalment not coming?

Common reasons: pending e-KYC, bank account not linked to Aadhaar, land record mismatch, or errors in name/IFSC. Check 'Know Your Status' on the portal.

Do tenant farmers get the benefit?

No. The land must be in the farmer's own name.

Where can I do e-KYC?

On pmkisan.gov.in via OTP, or biometric e-KYC at the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC).

Which instalment came last?

As per GKToday and Drishti (based on PIB) the 24th instalment was released on 30 October 2026. The date of the 25th is known only from a government announcement; keep checking pmkisan.gov.in.

Is e-KYC compulsory?

Yes, the portal says e-KYC is mandatory for registered farmers. If your instalment is stuck, complete it first.

What is needed to receive the instalment now?

As per Drishti land-record seeding, Aadhaar-bank linking and e-KYC must be completed; as per GKToday those who have not done e-KYC will not get the payment.

See each instalment and the status of 'e-KYC', 'Land Seeding' and 'Aadhaar Bank Account Seeding' (menu names may change). 4. If it is unclear, ask on helpline 155261 or 011-24300606.
