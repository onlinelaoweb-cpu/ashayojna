PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana

🌾 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Benefit₹6,000 per year
Instalments₹2,000 × 3 (roughly every 4 months)
PaymentDBT – direct to bank account
Official portalpmkisan.gov.in
⚠️ Instalment dates depend on government announcements, so no dates are given here. Check the latest status on pmkisan.gov.in.

What is the scheme?

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi is a Central Government scheme that gives eligible farmer families ₹6,000 a year as direct income support for farming needs. The money goes without any middleman into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Key benefits

  • ₹6,000 per year in total
  • Three instalments of ₹2,000, directly to the bank account (DBT)
  • Support for small farming costs such as seeds and fertiliser

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Indian citizen from a farmer family owning cultivable land
  • Land must be recorded in the farmer's own name in the state land records
  • 'Family' means husband, wife and minor children
  • For joint holdings, only one family member gets the benefit

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Institutional landholders (trusts, companies etc.)
  • Families where any member paid income tax in the last assessment year
  • Current or former ministers, MPs, MLAs, mayors
  • Serving or retired government employees (except Group D / MTS) and those with pension of ₹10,000 or more per month
  • Registered doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects
  • Tenant farmers who do not own land

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Land papers (khatauni / khasra / passbook)
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account (with IFSC)
  • Active mobile number (for OTP)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open pmkisan.gov.in and go to 'Farmers Corner'
  2. Choose 'New Farmer Registration' and enter Aadhaar and mobile number
  3. Fill state, land and bank details correctly and submit
  4. Complete e-KYC: enter Aadhaar in 'eKYC' and verify by OTP (if it fails, do biometric e-KYC at the nearest CSC)
  5. Keep checking 'Know Your Status'; village lists are under 'Beneficiary List'

Frequently asked questions

How much money does PM Kisan give?

₹6,000 a year to an eligible family, as three instalments of ₹2,000.

Why is my instalment not coming?

Common reasons: pending e-KYC, bank account not linked to Aadhaar, land record mismatch, or errors in name/IFSC. Check 'Know Your Status' on the portal.

Do tenant farmers get the benefit?

No. The land must be in the farmer's own name.

Where can I do e-KYC?

On pmkisan.gov.in via OTP, or biometric e-KYC at the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC).

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
