Griha Aadhar Scheme (Goa)

👩 📍 Goa · Directorate of Women & Child Development, Goa · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly amount₹1,500
WhoHousewives of Goa
Income limitReported up to ₹3 lakh a year
Portaldwcd.goa.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Griha Aadhar is a Goa Government scheme that gives housewives monthly financial aid to help run the household. Recent reports also mention a check on misuse and recovery, so giving correct documents is important.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,500 monthly financial aid
  • Payment into the bank account
  • There are reports of separate benefits for widows

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A woman who has lived in Goa for the fixed minimum years
  • A housewife with family income within the fixed limit
  • Aadhaar and a bank account

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Families with a government job or higher income
  • Applicants giving wrong information

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Goa residence/income certificate
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Get the application form from dwcd.goa.gov.in or the nearest service centre
  2. Fill the form and attach documents
  3. Verification follows after submission
dwcd.goa.gov.in – official department ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much is paid?

₹1,500 every month.

What is the income limit?

Reports say ₹3 lakh a year; confirm with the official department.
