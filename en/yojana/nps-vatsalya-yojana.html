NPS Vatsalya (pension account for children)

👶 🏛️ Central Government · PFRDA / Ministry of Finance · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Start with₹1,000
Minimum per year₹1,000
EligibleChildren below 18
Who runs the accountParent/guardian
⚠️ Withdrawal and annuity rules were inconsistent on one page (80% vs 40%). Check the latest FAQ on npstrust.org.in before opening.

What is the scheme?

In NPS Vatsalya a parent or guardian opens an NPS account in the name of a child below 18. At 18 the account can convert into a normal NPS account in the child's name.

Key benefits

  • Account can be opened with just ₹1,000, with no upper limit
  • Long-term savings and pension for the child
  • At 18, conversion to an NPS Tier-I All Citizen account or another scheme

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Indian, NRI and OCI children below 18
  • The account is opened and run by the parent/guardian, the child is the sole beneficiary

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • People aged 18 or more
  • Those who do not make the minimum yearly contribution

Documents required

  • Guardian's KYC (Aadhaar/PAN)
  • Child's birth certificate or age proof
  • Bank account details
  • Photo

How to apply (step by step)

  1. On the eNPS website choose NPS Vatsalya (Minors) and click 'Register Now'
  2. Verify the guardian by OTP and fill in the child's and guardian's details
  3. Pay ₹1,000 and authenticate by OTP/eSign; a PRAN is issued in the child's name
  4. Or visit a major bank, India Post or a PFRDA-registered centre

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum to deposit?

₹1,000 to open and at least ₹1,000 each year.

Can money be withdrawn before 18?

Limited partial withdrawal (up to 25% of contributions, for reasons like education or serious illness) is allowed after 3 years, as reported.

What happens at 18?

The account becomes a normal NPS account in the child's name and fresh KYC is required within three months.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
