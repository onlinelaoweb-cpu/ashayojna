Sarvajan Pension Yojana (Jharkhand)

👴 📍 Jharkhand · Dept of Women, Child Development & Social Security, Jharkhand · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Pension₹1,000 a month
WhoEligible elderly, widows, disabled etc.
PaymentInto the bank account
Portalsarvajanpension.jharkhand.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Sarvajan Pension is Jharkhand's social security pension giving eligible people ₹1,000 a month. The state's 2026 budget is reported to focus on pensions and direct cash support.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,000 pension every month
  • One portal for several categories
  • Money in the bank account

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A resident of Jharkhand
  • Meeting the category condition (such as age, widow, disability)
  • The fixed income/poverty conditions

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those already taking another pension
  • Those ineligible on the income condition

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Age/category proof
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open sarvajanpension.jharkhand.gov.in
  2. Fill the application and attach documents
  3. Pension starts after verification
sarvajanpension.jharkhand.gov.in – official portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much pension is paid?

₹1,000 a month.

Where to confirm eligibility?

From the official portal or the block office.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
