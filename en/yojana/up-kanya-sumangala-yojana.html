Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana (Uttar Pradesh)

👧 📍 Uttar Pradesh · Women Welfare Department, Uttar Pradesh · Last checked: 11 November 2026

Total support₹25,000 (in six stages)
Income limitAnnual family income up to ₹3 lakh
Benefit forMaximum 2 daughters per family
Helpline1800-180-1111
⚠️ Stage-wise instalment amounts and conditions have changed over time. Check the latest rules at mksy.up.gov.in before applying.

What is the scheme?

Kanya Sumangala is an Uttar Pradesh Government scheme that gives financial help in instalments, directly to the bank account (DBT), at different stages from a girl's birth to graduation/diploma. Its aim is to promote the birth and education of daughters.

Key benefits

  • Total ₹25,000 support (recently raised from ₹15,000)
  • Six stages: at birth, at one year's vaccination, on admission to class 1, class 6, class 9, and on admission to graduation/diploma after class 12
  • Each stage's amount goes directly to the bank account (DBT)
  • The stage-wise amounts are fixed by government order; see the latest table at mksy.up.gov.in

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Family is a permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh
  • Annual family income ₹3 lakh or less
  • Benefit for a maximum of 2 daughters per family (adopted daughter also included, with rules)
  • Girl's birth certificate and a bank account are required

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those with annual income above ₹3 lakh
  • Families with more than two daughters (special cases like twins have separate rules)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card of parent/guardian
  • Girl's birth certificate
  • Bank account details (active for DBT)
  • UP residence/address proof
  • Income certificate
  • Proof of school admission (for education-linked stages)
  • Affidavit/declaration and photo

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Open mksy.up.gov.in and go to the citizen service portal / 'Apply Here'
  2. Register with name, mobile and Aadhaar and verify by OTP
  3. Log in, fill the girl's and bank details and upload documents
  4. Submit and keep the application number safe
  5. For offline application contact the BDO/SDM office or the District Probation Officer
mksy.up.gov.in – official portal ↗

📞 Toll-free: 1800-180-1111

Frequently asked questions

How much money does Kanya Sumangala give in total?

₹25,000 in six stages, from the girl's birth to graduation. See the latest table on the portal for stage-wise amounts.

What is the income limit?

Annual family income up to ₹3 lakh.

Will a girl from another state get it?

No, the family must be a permanent resident of Uttar Pradesh.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
