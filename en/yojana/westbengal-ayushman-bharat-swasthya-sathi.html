Ayushman Bharat in West Bengal (replacing Swasthya Sathi)

📤 Share:

🏥 📍 West Bengal · Health & Family Welfare Department, West Bengal · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Earlier schemeSwasthya Sathi: about 2.42 crore families
New arrangementAyushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
Cover₹5 lakh per family (normal Ayushman)
StatusChange reported, details incomplete
⚠️ The change is based on one news report. Its implementation status and eligibility could not be checked here; confirm with the hospital/block health office.

What is the scheme?

After a new government took office in West Bengal in May 2026, reports said Ayushman Bharat would replace the state's Swasthya Sathi scheme. The number of eligible families may be lower than under Swasthya Sathi.

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (general rule)
  • Treatment at listed government and private hospitals

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Bengal families who qualify under Ayushman Bharat (SECC list etc.)
  • An Ayushman card
  • Not every Swasthya Sathi family may be eligible in the new system (per reports)

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who do not meet the Ayushman eligibility
  • Those without a card

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Ayushman card
  • ID proof

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check eligibility under 'Am I Eligible' on pmjay.gov.in
  2. If eligible, get the Ayushman card made
  3. Show the card at a listed hospital for treatment
  4. Swasthya Sathi card holders should ask their hospital/block health office about the change
pmjay.gov.in – Ayushman Bharat portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

Has Swasthya Sathi been closed?

Reports say Ayushman Bharat is being introduced in its place. The official notification status could not be checked here.

How do I check eligibility?

Through 'Am I Eligible' on pmjay.gov.in.

How much cover will I get?

Generally ₹5 lakh per family under Ayushman Bharat.
