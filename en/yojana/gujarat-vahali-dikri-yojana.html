Vahali Dikri Yojana (Gujarat)

🎀 📍 Gujarat · Women & Child Development Department, Gujarat · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Total help₹1,10,000
First instalment₹4,000 (admission to Class 1)
Second instalment₹6,000 (admission to Class 9)
Third instalment₹1,00,000 (higher education or marriage)
⚠️ The birth-date condition and income limit may change over time. Confirm with the department before applying.

What is the scheme?

Vahali Dikri Yojana is a Gujarat Government scheme for girls. An eligible family's daughter gets a total of ₹1,10,000 at different stages of her schooling.

Key benefits

  • ₹4,000 when the girl is admitted to Class 1
  • ₹6,000 on admission to Class 9
  • ₹1,00,000 after she passes Class 12 and goes for higher education, or at marriage

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Girl born after 2 August 2019
  • Annual family income below ₹2 lakh
  • Benefit for a maximum of two living daughters in a family
  • Resident of Gujarat

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Girls born before the cut-off date
  • Families above the income limit
  • More than two daughters in the family

Documents required

  • Proof of Gujarat residence
  • Girl's Aadhaar card
  • Recent passport-size photo
  • Family income certificate
  • Bank account details
  • Birth certificate

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Get the form from an Anganwadi centre or district Women & Child Development office
  2. Fill the form and attach documents
  3. Submit at the same office
  4. After verification a list of selected girls is published and help is given in three stages
wcd.gujarat.gov.in – Women & Child Development Dept ↗

Frequently asked questions

What is the total amount?

₹1,10,000 in three stages.

How many daughters get the benefit?

A maximum of two living daughters in a family.

Where to apply?

At an Anganwadi centre or district Women & Child Development office.
