Shakti Yojana (Karnataka)

🚌 📍 Karnataka · Transport Department, Karnataka · Last checked: 15 November 2026

BenefitFree travel on KSRTC buses
IDAadhaar card
ForWomen residents of Karnataka
StatusPlan to issue passes to eligible residents (report)
⚠️ Because of reports of a review, pass and bus rules may change. Confirm with the Transport Department or Seva Sindhu.

What is the scheme?

Under Shakti Yojana, women of Karnataka get free travel on state transport buses by showing Aadhaar. A July 2026 report says the government is reviewing the scheme to issue passes only to eligible Karnataka residents.

Key benefits

  • Free travel for women on designated KSRTC buses
  • Travel by showing the Aadhaar card

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Woman resident of Karnataka
  • Aadhaar card
  • See the Transport Department/Seva Sindhu for exact rules

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Tourists coming from other states (per reports)
  • Those who are not eligible residents

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Residence proof
  • Photo (for the pass)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Check the Transport Department or Seva Sindhu for pass application/rules
  2. Apply with Aadhaar and residence proof (if the pass system is in place)
  3. Show your Aadhaar or pass when boarding the bus
sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in – Seva Sindhu ↗

Frequently asked questions

Who is Shakti Yojana for?

Women residents of Karnataka.

Will a pass be required?

A July 2026 report says passes are planned for eligible residents. Rules may change; please confirm.

Which buses are covered?

Designated ordinary KSRTC buses; see the Transport Department for the exact list.
