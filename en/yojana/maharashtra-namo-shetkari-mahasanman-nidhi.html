Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana (Maharashtra)

🌾 📍 Maharashtra · Agriculture Department, Maharashtra · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Yearly amount₹6,000
InstalmentsThree of ₹2,000
ConditionPM Kisan beneficiary farmer
ApplicationNo separate application
⚠️ Instalment timing was unclear in one source and dates depend on government announcements. Confirm with the District Agriculture Officer.

What is the scheme?

Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi is a Maharashtra Government scheme giving PM Kisan Samman Nidhi beneficiary farmers ₹6,000 a year from the state. The money comes to the same Aadhaar-linked bank account that gets PM Kisan.

Key benefits

  • ₹6,000 a year in three instalments of ₹2,000
  • With PM Kisan's ₹6,000, a total of about ₹12,000 a year
  • No separate application needed

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Maharashtra farmers who are PM Kisan Samman Nidhi beneficiaries
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account (a must)

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those not registered under PM Kisan
  • Those whose PM Kisan e-KYC/Aadhaar seeding is incomplete

Documents required

  • PM Kisan registration number
  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Make sure you are registered under PM Kisan and e-KYC is done
  2. No separate application, eligible farmers get the money automatically
  3. Check status on the official Namo Shetkari portal or with the District Agriculture Officer

Frequently asked questions

How much money is given?

₹6,000 a year in three instalments.

Is a separate application needed?

No, PM Kisan registered farmers are automatically eligible.

When does the instalment come?

Dates depend on government announcements, so none is given here.
