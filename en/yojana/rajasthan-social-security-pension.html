Rajasthan Social Security Pension

👵 📍 Rajasthan · Social Justice & Empowerment Department, Rajasthan · Last checked: 12 November 2026

Monthly pension₹1,450 (2026-27 budget announcement)
BeneficiariesOver 91 lakh
WhoElderly, widow, single woman, disabled, small/marginal farmer
Applye-Mitra or mobile app
⚠️ The ₹1,450 figure is from a news report on the budget announcement and the exact effective date was not found. Confirm category-wise conditions with the department.

What is the scheme?

Under Rajasthan's Social Security Pension, eligible elderly people, widows, single women, disabled persons and small and marginal farmers get a monthly pension directly in their bank account. A March 2026 report says the amount was raised to ₹1,450 a month.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,450 a month pension (per a March 2026 report), by DBT to an Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Application is digital and paperless, via e-Mitra or mobile app
  • Annual verification made easy by biometric or OTP

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Resident of Rajasthan
  • Belongs to a category: elderly, widow, single woman, disabled or small/marginal farmer
  • Exact age and income conditions differ by category, so confirm with the department
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those who do not belong to an eligible category
  • Those found ineligible on verification

Documents required

  • Jan Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar card
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Category proof (age, widowhood, disability etc.)
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Visit a nearby e-Mitra centre or use the department's mobile app
  2. Apply online stating your category
  3. After sanction, complete the yearly verification by biometric/OTP
sje.rajasthan.gov.in – Social Justice Department ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much pension does Rajasthan give now?

As per a March 2026 report, ₹1,450 a month. Earlier it was ₹1,300 in January 2026.

Where to apply?

At an e-Mitra centre or through the mobile app.

Who gets it?

Elderly, widows, single women, disabled persons and small/marginal farmers.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
