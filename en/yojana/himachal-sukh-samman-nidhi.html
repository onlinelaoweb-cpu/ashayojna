Indira Gandhi Pyari Behna Sukh Samman Nidhi (Himachal)

👩 📍 Himachal Pradesh · Government of Himachal Pradesh · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly amount₹1,500
Age18 to 59 years
Per familyOne woman
Portalhimachalservices.nic.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

This is a Himachal Pradesh Government scheme giving eligible women ₹1,500 a month. As per reports only one woman per family gets it, and ineligible beneficiaries may have to return the money.

Key benefits

  • ₹1,500 monthly financial aid
  • Money goes directly to the bank account
  • Separate announcements have been made for some districts/areas

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A woman resident of Himachal Pradesh
  • Age 18 to 59 years (as per reports)
  • Only one woman per family is eligible

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Other women of the same family
  • Those ineligible under income/government job conditions

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Himachal domicile/residence proof
  • Bank account details (linked to Aadhaar)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to himachalservices.nic.in or the nearest Lokmitra/CSC
  2. Fill the scheme application form
  3. Submit with documents and wait for verification
himachalservices.nic.in – state services portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much is paid?

₹1,500 every month.

Will all women in a family get it?

No, as per reports one woman per family.
