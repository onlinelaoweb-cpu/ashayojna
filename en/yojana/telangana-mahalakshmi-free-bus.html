Mahalakshmi – Free Bus Travel for Women (Telangana)

🚌 📍 Telangana · Transport Dept / TGSRTC, Telangana · Last checked: 15 November 2026

BenefitFree bus ride (zero ticket)
Started9 December 2023
Which busesPalle Velugu, City Ordinary, Express, Metro Express (report)
IDAadhaar/government photo ID; a smart card has also been announced
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

Under the Mahalakshmi scheme, women in Telangana do not pay fare in some categories of buses of the state transport corporation (TGSRTC). The conductor issues a 'zero ticket' and the government pays the fare amount to the corporation. As per reports the scheme continues in 2026, and in February 2026 a Mahalakshmi smart card was announced for identification.

Key benefits

  • No fare in eligible buses
  • A zero ticket is issued on every trip
  • Saves money on daily travel, work and study trips

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Women and girls of Telangana
  • Travel in an eligible bus category (Palle Velugu, City Ordinary, Express, Metro Express)
  • Carry a valid ID

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Other bus categories such as AC/luxury (reported as separate; confirm with TGSRTC)
  • Trips going outside the state (confirm)

Documents required

  • Aadhaar or another valid government photo ID
  • Aadhaar and a photo for the smart card (if launched in your area)

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Board an eligible bus
  2. Show your ID to the conductor (or the smart card, if it applies in your area)
  3. Take the zero ticket and keep it till the trip ends
  4. Check the latest smart card status from TGSRTC or government announcements
telangana.gov.in – Telangana Government main portal ↗

Frequently asked questions

Is travel free in every bus?

No. As per reports it is free in Palle Velugu, City Ordinary, Express and Metro Express buses.

Which ID is needed?

Usually Aadhaar or a government photo ID. A smart card has been announced, but confirm with TGSRTC where and from when it applies.
