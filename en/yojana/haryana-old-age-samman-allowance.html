Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance / Social Security Pension

👵 📍 Haryana · Department of Social Justice, Empowerment, SC & BC Welfare and Antyodaya (SEWA), Haryana · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Monthly pension₹3,200
Effective from1 November 2025
WhoElderly, widows, disabled, destitute women etc.
Portalsewa.haryana.gov.in
⚠️ The income limit and age conditions could not be checked here. The amount is based on news reports; confirm on the SEWA portal before applying.

What is the scheme?

The Haryana Government raised the social security pension by ₹200, from ₹3,000 to ₹3,200 a month. It covers the Old Age Samman Allowance, Ladli social security allowance, disability pension and help for widows, destitute women, widowers, unmarried persons, cancer patients and rare disease patients.

Key benefits

  • ₹3,200 a month (new rate from 1 November 2025, per reports)
  • Categories such as Old Age Samman Allowance, disabled, widows and destitute women are covered
  • Allowance for non-school-going disabled children raised from ₹2,400 to ₹2,600 and help for Kashmiri migrant families also increased (per reports)

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Resident of Haryana
  • Elderly, widow, disabled or another eligible category
  • Exact age and income conditions differ by category; see the SEWA department notification or portal

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Those not in an eligible category
  • Those found ineligible on verification

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Age/category proof
  • Income proof
  • Bank account details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to sewa.haryana.gov.in and choose the pension scheme for your category
  2. Fill the online application or get it done at a nearby Saral/CSC centre
  3. Upload documents and check the application status on the portal
sewa.haryana.gov.in – SEWA portal ↗
