Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi – KASP (Kerala)

🏥 📍 Kerala · State Health Agency, Kerala · Last checked: 15 November 2026

Cover₹5 lakh per family per year
TreatmentCashless, at empanelled hospitals
Pre-existingCovered from day one
Portalsha.kerala.gov.in
⚠️ Amounts and rules can change, and this is based on reports. Confirm on the official portal or helpline before applying.

What is the scheme?

KASP is Kerala's government health insurance scheme. An eligible family gets cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh a year in empanelled government and private hospitals. All sources agree on the ₹5 lakh cover, but they describe eligibility differently, so check your name with the State Health Agency or at a hospital help desk.

Key benefits

  • Cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family a year
  • Pre-existing diseases covered from day one
  • Costs for 3 days before and 15 days after admission are also reported as covered
  • No age or family-size limit is reported

Eligibility – who can apply

  • A resident of Kerala
  • A family in an eligible ration card category (priority/AAY) or on the government list; some sources mention an income limit of ₹3 lakh
  • All family members are covered

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Residents of other states
  • Families that do not meet the list/income condition

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Ration card
  • Income certificate (where asked)
  • Mobile number

How to apply (step by step)

  1. Go to the KASP help desk of an empanelled government/private hospital with your ration card and Aadhaar
  2. Get eligibility checked and identity verified
  3. Get your card/e-card made
  4. Only empanelled hospitals are covered, so confirm before treatment
sha.kerala.gov.in – State Health Agency, Kerala ↗

Frequently asked questions

How much cover is there?

₹5 lakh per family per year, cashless.

Is it valid in every hospital?

No, only in empanelled hospitals. See the list on sha.kerala.gov.in.

What is the helpline number?

Sources gave different numbers, so none is given here. Take the number from the official portal.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
