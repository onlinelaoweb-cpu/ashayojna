e-Shram Card (for unorganised workers)

🪪 🏛️ Central Government · Ministry of Labour & Employment · Last checked: 11 November 2026

Age16 to 59 years
ID12-digit unique UAN
FeeRegistration is free
Helpline14434 / 1800-889-6811
⚠️ Benefits and eligibility rules linked to e-Shram keep changing. Check the latest list on eshram.gov.in or the helpline.

What is the scheme?

The e-Shram portal is the national database of unorganised workers (farm labourers, construction workers, domestic workers, vendors, drivers etc.). After registering you get an e-Shram card with a 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN), which makes it easier to reach government social security schemes.

Key benefits

  • A permanent 12-digit UAN and card valid across India
  • Easier to join social security schemes
  • Provision for benefits like accident insurance through the portal (as per terms; see the latest benefits on the portal)
  • Information on employment and skill schemes

Eligibility – who can apply

  • Unorganised sector worker aged 16 to 59
  • Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
  • Not an income taxpayer
  • Not a member of EPFO/ESIC (see latest rules on the portal)

Who is not eligible / things to note

  • Income taxpayers
  • Members of EPFO or ESIC
  • People above 59 years

Documents required

  • Aadhaar card
  • Mobile number linked to Aadhaar
  • Bank account details

How to apply (step by step)

  1. On eshram.gov.in choose 'Register on eShram'
  2. Enter the Aadhaar-linked mobile number and captcha and get an OTP
  3. Enter the OTP to complete Aadhaar e-KYC
  4. Fill address, education, occupation, skill and bank details
  5. Submit the self-declaration and download the e-Shram card (registration can also be done at a CSC)
eshram.gov.in – official portal ↗

📞 Toll-free: 14434 or 1800-889-6811 (9 am to 6 pm)

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fee for the e-Shram card?

Self-registration on the portal is free. A CSC may charge a small service fee. Do not pay extra in the name of a government fee.

Who cannot get it?

Income taxpayers and EPFO/ESIC members are not eligible, and age must be 16 to 59.

What is a UAN?

The permanent 12-digit number you get after e-Shram registration, which works as identification across India.

Beware: Do not pay any agent or website in the name of applying for a government scheme. Applying on the official portal is generally free. Do not share OTP, PIN or confidential bank details with anyone.
