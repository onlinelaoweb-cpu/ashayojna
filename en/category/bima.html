🛡️ Insurance & Pension

Low-premium protection and old-age pension

🏦Bank account

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

Zero-balance bank account with a RuPay card and accident insurance.

Full details →🛡️Life insurance

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

₹2 lakh life insurance for ₹436 a year.

Full details →🩹Accident insurance

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh for just ₹20 a year.

Full details →👴Pension

Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

A fixed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month after age 60.

Full details →🪪Worker registration

e-Shram Card (for unorganised workers)

National registration of unorganised workers, a 12-digit UAN and the route to social security schemes.

Full details →👵UP · Pension

Uttar Pradesh Pension Scheme – Old Age, Widow & Disability (SSPY)

₹1,000 a month pension for eligible elderly, destitute women and disabled persons in Uttar Pradesh.

Full details →👴Bihar · Pension

Bihar Social Security Pension – ₹1,100 a month (elderly, widow, disabled)

₹1,100 a month pension for the elderly, widows and disabled in Bihar; e-KYC required.

Full details →👴MP · Pension

Madhya Pradesh Samagra Samajik Suraksha Pension Yojana

₹600 a month pension for eligible elderly, widows, abandoned women and disabled persons in Madhya Pradesh.

Full details →🧒Rajasthan · Child support

Palanhar Yojana (Rajasthan)

Monthly support to families caring for orphan and needy children.

Full details →👵Rajasthan · Pension

Rajasthan Social Security Pension

₹1,450 a month for the elderly, widows, single women, disabled persons and small farmers (March 2026 announcement).

Full details →👵Delhi · Pension

Delhi Old Age Pension Scheme

₹2,000 a month for ages 60 to 69, ₹2,500 for 70+; extra ₹500 for SC/ST/minorities.

Full details →👷Central · Pension

Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM)

Assured pension of at least ₹3,000 a month after age 60 for unorganised workers.

Full details →👶Central · Children

NPS Vatsalya (pension account for children)

Parents can open an NPS account in a child's name from just ₹1,000 to start saving for the future.

Full details →👵Haryana · Pension

Haryana Old Age Samman Allowance / Social Security Pension

Social security pension in Haryana raised to ₹3,200 a month (from 1 November 2025).

Full details →👴Andhra Pradesh · Pension

NTR Bharosa Pension (Andhra Pradesh)

Monthly social security pension for senior citizens, widows, disabled people and others, ₹4,000 in most categories.

Full details →👴Jharkhand · Pension

Sarvajan Pension Yojana (Jharkhand)

₹1,000 a month pension for eligible people of Jharkhand, across several categories.

Full details →👴Kerala · Pension

Kerala Social Security Welfare Pension

Monthly welfare pension for seniors, widows, disabled people and farmers in Kerala.

Full details →👴Sikkim · Pension

Sikkim Social Security Pension

Portal for state and national pension schemes for seniors, widows and disabled people in Sikkim.

Full details →👴Tripura · Pension

Mukhyamantri Samajik Sahayata Prakalpa (Tripura Pension)

State pension schemes for the old, widows, disabled and unorganised workers in Tripura.

Full details →👵Goa · Pension

Dayanand Social Security Scheme – DSSS (Goa)

Monthly social security assistance in Goa for senior citizens, widows, disabled persons, single women and others; the amount depends on category.

Full details →👴Central · Old-age pension

Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension (IGNOAPS)

Central pension for BPL senior citizens aged 60+: ₹200 a month for 60-79 and ₹500 for 80+; many states add a top-up.

Full details →🕊️Central · Family support

National Family Benefit Scheme – NFBS (₹20,000 One-time)

A one-time ₹20,000 help to a BPL family when its main earning member dies; some states give more.
