👧 Women & Girl Schemes

Savings and support schemes for girls and women

👧Daughter

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

A government savings scheme for a daughter's future, with a high interest rate and tax benefits.

Full details →🤱Maternity support

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

₹5,000 for the first child and ₹6,000 if the second child is a girl.

Full details →👧UP · Daughter

Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana (Uttar Pradesh)

Total ₹25,000 support for Uttar Pradesh daughters in six stages, from birth to graduation.

Full details →💍UP · Marriage support

Mukhyamantri Samuhik Vivah Yojana (Uttar Pradesh)

Up to ₹1 lakh support for eligible couples in mass marriages (₹60,000 in the bride's account).

Full details →👩MP · Women

Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

₹1,500 a month for eligible women of Madhya Pradesh. New registrations are reported to be closed for now.

Full details →🎀MP · Daughter

Ladli Laxmi Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

Stage-wise financial help for girls from birth, through schooling, up to age 21.

Full details →🌸Rajasthan · Daughter

Mukhyamantri Rajshri Yojana (Rajasthan)

Total ₹50,000 in six stages for a girl from birth to class 12. Lado Protsahan Yojana for newer births.

Full details →💍MP · Marriage

Mukhyamantri Kanya Vivah Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

Financial help to the bride when marrying at a government-organised mass wedding.

Full details →👩Maharashtra · Women

Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana (Maharashtra)

₹1,500 a month for eligible women of Maharashtra. Beneficiary list rebuilt after e-KYC.

Full details →🎀Maharashtra · Daughter

Majhi Kanya Bhagyashree Yojana (Maharashtra)

₹50,000 fixed deposit for one daughter, ₹25,000 each for two daughters.

Full details →🎀Gujarat · Daughter

Vahali Dikri Yojana (Gujarat)

Total ₹1,10,000 for a girl in three stages.

Full details →💍Gujarat · Marriage

Kunvarbai Nu Mameru Yojana (Gujarat)

₹12,000 help at the marriage of a Scheduled Tribe daughter.

Full details →👩Delhi · Women

Delhi Laxmi Yojana (earlier Mahila Samriddhi Yojana)

₹2,500 a month for eligible women of Delhi.

Full details →🎀Delhi · Daughter

Lakhpati Bitiya Yojana (Delhi)

Replaces the old Ladli scheme: ₹56,000 deposited in stages from birth to higher education, more than ₹1 lakh at maturity.

Full details →👩Haryana · Women

Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana (Haryana)

₹2,100 a month for eligible women of Haryana: ₹1,100 direct and ₹1,000 into a deposit.

Full details →👩Karnataka · Women

Gruha Lakshmi Yojana (Karnataka)

₹2,000 a month for the woman head of the family. Reports mention pending payments and a review.

Full details →🚌Karnataka · Women

Shakti Yojana (Karnataka)

Free travel for women of Karnataka on KSRTC ordinary buses, using Aadhaar.

Full details →👩West Bengal · Women

Annapurna Bhandar Yojana (West Bengal, earlier Lakshmir Bhandar)

Reported ₹3,000 a month for women; said to replace the earlier Lakshmir Bhandar.

Full details →👩Tamil Nadu · Women

Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai (Tamil Nadu)

₹1,000 a month for the woman head of the family; ₹2,500 mentioned under the new government but unconfirmed.

Full details →👩Assam · Women

Orunodoi Scheme (Assam)

Monthly direct bank transfer to the woman head of needy families in Assam.

Full details →👩Chhattisgarh · Women

Mahtari Vandan Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

₹1,000 every month directly to eligible married women of Chhattisgarh.

Full details →👩Goa · Women

Griha Aadhar Scheme (Goa)

Monthly aid of ₹1,500 to housewives in Goa, subject to an income condition.

Full details →👩Himachal Pradesh · Women

Indira Gandhi Pyari Behna Sukh Samman Nidhi (Himachal)

₹1,500 a month for eligible women of Himachal Pradesh; only one woman per family gets it.

Full details →👩Jharkhand · Women

Mukhyamantri Maiyan Samman Yojana (Jharkhand)

₹2,500 every month directly to eligible women of Jharkhand.

Full details →👩Odisha · Women

Subhadra Yojana (Odisha)

₹10,000 a year to eligible women of Odisha, in two instalments directly to the bank account.

Full details →🎀Uttarakhand · Girl child

Nanda Gaura Devi Kanya Dhan Yojana (Uttarakhand)

Financial help on the birth and education of a girl in Uttarakhand; reports mention about ₹50,000 after passing Class 12.
