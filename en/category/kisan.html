🌾 Farmer Schemes

Direct income support and cheap credit for farmers

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana

₹6,000 a year to eligible farmer families, in three instalments straight to the bank account.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Insurance for crop loss from natural calamities, with the farmer paying only 1.5% to 5% premium.

Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)

₹4,000 a year extra from the state for farmers registered under PM Kisan.

Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana (Maharashtra)

₹6,000 a year from the state for PM Kisan farmers of Maharashtra.

PM-KUSUM (Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan)

Heavy subsidy on solar pumps for farmers; the farmer's share is about 10%.

Annadata Sukhibhava (Andhra Pradesh)

Up to ₹20,000 a year for farmers, reported to include the PM-KISAN share.

Krishak Unnati Yojana (Chhattisgarh)

Input assistance above MSP for registered paddy-selling farmers; about ₹10,324 crore was released in 2026.

CM-KISAN Yojana (Odisha)

₹4,000 a year of help for Odisha farmer families; it replaced the KALIA scheme.
