🏭 PMEGP Subsidy Calculator

Enter the project cost and choose your category. You will see the approximate subsidy, your contribution and the bank loan.

⚠️ This is only an estimate. Subsidy rates and limits are based on a financial guide and can change. The actual amount depends on the bank's approval and the official portal. Confirm at kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal.

Subsidy rates (as per reports)

  • General category: 15% in urban and 25% in rural areas.
  • Special category: 25% in urban and 35% in rural areas.
  • Own contribution: 10% for general and 5% for special (as per reports).
  • Maximum project cost: ₹50 lakh for manufacturing and ₹20 lakh for service/trading.
