Central Government schemes (for all of India)

🌾Farmers

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana

₹6,000 a year to eligible farmer families, in three instalments straight to the bank account.

Full details →🏥Health

Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY (free treatment up to ₹5 lakh)

Cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh a year for eligible families. A separate Vay Vandana card for those aged 70+.

Full details →🔥Home & Energy

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

Free LPG connection for women from poor families and a ₹300 subsidy per cylinder.

Full details →👧Daughter

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

A government savings scheme for a daughter's future, with a high interest rate and tax benefits.

Full details →☀️Solar

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Central subsidy for rooftop solar panels, up to ₹78,000.

Full details →💼Loan

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

Collateral-free loans for small businesses, up to ₹20 lakh.

Full details →🏦Bank account

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

Zero-balance bank account with a RuPay card and accident insurance.

Full details →🛡️Life insurance

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

₹2 lakh life insurance for ₹436 a year.

Full details →🩹Accident insurance

Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh for just ₹20 a year.

Full details →👴Pension

Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

A fixed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month after age 60.

Full details →💳Farm loan

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Cheap and timely credit for farmers for cultivation.

Full details →🏡Housing

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)

Assistance up to ₹1.20 lakh (₹1.30 lakh in hilly/North-East areas) to build a pucca house in rural areas.

Full details →🏘️Urban housing

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)

Assistance up to ₹2.50 lakh to build/buy a house, or interest subsidy on home loans, for urban families.

Full details →🛠️Artisans

PM Vishwakarma Yojana

Training, ₹15,000 toolkit support and cheap loans for traditional artisans.

Full details →🛒Street vendor

PM SVANidhi Yojana (for street vendors)

Working-capital loans in three tranches for street vendors, scheme extended to 2030.

Full details →🌦️Crop insurance

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Insurance for crop loss from natural calamities, with the farmer paying only 1.5% to 5% premium.

Full details →🤱Maternity support

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

₹5,000 for the first child and ₹6,000 if the second child is a girl.

Full details →🪪Worker registration

e-Shram Card (for unorganised workers)

National registration of unorganised workers, a 12-digit UAN and the route to social security schemes.

Full details →🌾Free ration

Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (free ration)

Free monthly grain for eligible ration card holders, scheme extended to December 2028.
